2. SEPARATE THREE DIFFERENT USES OF MICROSTRUCTURE INFORMATION

A. DIRECTIONAL ALPHA
    predicts future NQ movement large enough to trade standalone.

B. SELECTIVITY / META-LABELING
    improves hit rate or payoff of a larger structural setup.

C. EXECUTION ALPHA
    improves entry timing / reduces adverse selection / reduces realized
    trading cost without necessarily predicting a large standalone move.

Evaluate all three separately.

A 0.5-tick informational signal may be useless as a standalone trade
but economically important as an execution overlay.
