========================================================================

External literature provides a useful PRIOR, not proof for NQ.

Known broad findings worth investigating include:

A. Order-flow imbalance can explain very short-horizon price movements.

B. Bid/ask queue imbalance can predict the direction of the next
   mid-price movement.

   top-of-book alone.

E. Short-horizon predictability often DECAYS very quickly.

F. Statistical predictability frequently FAILS to survive retail
   transaction costs.

G. Machine-learning forecast accuracy does NOT imply tradable PnL.

H. Market microstructure relationships can vary strongly by:
       instrument
       session
       spread
       liquidity
       matching mechanism
       volatility regime.

    Can we predict the next tick?

It is:

    Can we identify states whose EXPECTED FORWARD MOVE
    is sufficiently larger than total executable friction?
