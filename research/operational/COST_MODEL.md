## §1 THE RULE THAT WAS MISSING

> **Every cost figure must declare whether it is `COMMISSION_ONLY`, `SPREAD_ONLY` or `ALL_IN`.**

`SPREAD_ONLY` figures are **addends** onto a commission-inclusive net. `ALL_IN` figures are
**totals**. Storing an addend and a total in the same column is what produced a published
arithmetic error, and it produced two numeral collisions that are still live traps:

| collision | one meaning | the other |
|---|---|---|
| **$14.36 vs $14.44** | scalping-lab **C1, ALL_IN** ($4.36 + 1 tick/side) | **P1 spread, SPREAD_ONLY** |
| **$24.00 twice** | W82 pessimistic **SPREAD bound** | `G3_XMLAT_01` **ALL_IN** stress rung |

Eight cents apart, on opposite sides of the commission line. A second tag is mandatory too:

> **Every figure must declare `MEASURED` (with `n` and coverage) / `MODELLED` / `BOUND` / `ASSUMED`.**

Coverage matters more than it looks: **both "measured" spreads rest on under 9 % of their
book's contract round turns**, and on one price era.

## §3 WHERE COST IS APPLIED IN CODE

Commission and spread are charged in **different places**, which is itself a trap:

- **Commission — inside the fill engine, per contract, at trade close.**
  `research/weekly_edge/src/run_we_w98.py:74,87` — `pnl = … - COMM_RT * u`, `COMM_RT = 4.36`.
- **Spread — post-hoc, once per contract round turn, never inside the engine.**
  `research/weekly_edge/src/we_lab.py:161-180` — `rate()` builds a contract-weighted histogram
  over the entry *and* exit minute of every trade against W82's committed per-minute profile.
- **XM is the exception**: `run_we_w102.py:157` charges `COMM_RT + spread` in **one** constant
  ($16.86) *inside* the P&L column. The only place the two are combined.
- ⚠️ **`research_sdk/champion_eval.py:55` declares `COMM_RT = 4.36` and never uses it.** That
  evaluator applies **no cost at all** — it consumes an already-costed ledger. Latent trap.
- **NinjaScript `CommissionRT = 4.36`** feeds only the session box and `realizedPnl`. It is
  **decision-inert**, and deliberately left at the NQ value in the MNQ port — the W98 box is
  per-contract on the *primary's* PointValue. Correct, not a defect.

## §5 🔴 THE ONE UNMEASURED INPUT — and it is the live book's

The live book executes in **MNQ**. Its shipped friction estimate is *"≈ $35/wk at 3 MNQ"*, built on:

> *"Spread does not degrade (same 0.25 tick, point value scales exactly 1/10)."*

**The arithmetic is right and the premise is unverified.** NQ and MNQ share a 0.25-point tick, so
one MNQ tick costs exactly 1/10 of one NQ tick per unit of index exposure — *if both quote the
same number of ticks*. **Nobody has measured whether they do.**

## §6 STANDING RULES

1. **Never subtract commission twice.** Ask what the baseline already nets before deducting.
2. **Never call a spread figure "all-in".** That one word produced a wrong decomposition in a
   headline table — and then a wrong *correction*, because the follow-up claimed a $59/wk impact
   that two cancelling errors had already netted to zero. **Mislabels propagate into their own fixes.**
3. **Name the instrument.** NQ and MNQ have different commissions and the live book is MNQ.
4. **Quote coverage with every measured spread.** 5.1 % and 8.7 % are not "measured" in the sense
   a reader assumes.
5. **Era-tag every spread.** 2025H2 and 2026 Jan–May ran $17.12; 2026 Jun–Jul ran $28.69.
6. **A research headline and an NT8 net are not the same quantity** — NT8 charges the template and
   zero slippage; research charges commission plus a modelled spread.
