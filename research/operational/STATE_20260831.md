index is normalised by **its own** 60-session sigma; today's observation is appended **after** use.
Stale feeds disqualify the session; half-days are declined. ~34 % of sessions qualify.

**⚠️ There is NO established mechanism for P1** — `CURRENT_BASELINE.md:58` labels it
