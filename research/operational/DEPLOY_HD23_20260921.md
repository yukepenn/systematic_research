## 6 · ⭐ ADDENDUM 2026-09-05 — `HD-24`, and a **CHANGED RECOMMENDATION** for this account

**The owner trades MNQ by hand on `2047681`. That is a standing fact, not an exception**, so
"do not do that" is not an available control. Two things changed as a result.

### 6.1 The 2026-09-03 conclusion was too strong, and is revised

That write-up said *"ENFORCE is correct ONLY on an account no human trades by hand."* The
reason it gave was that the account is a **three-way** sum — this leg + the other leg + the
human. **XM's withdrawal on 2026-09-05 made it a two-way sum**, and for a two-way sum the
clamp's guarantee is **one-sided and needs no attribution at all**:

> **An exit may never take the ACCOUNT across flat against me.**
> `safe = max(0, min(want, accountInMyDirection))` can only ever submit **fewer** contracts than
> asked, so it can never **create** an unowned position — whoever removed the original.
> A hand-flattened account makes the exit submit **zero**, which is exactly right, and is
> precisely what would have prevented 2026-09-03.

Residual cost, stated: a wrong or stale account read could leave a real position partly open.
That is why ENFORCE also **Halts and logs at ERROR** — the owner is told, not silently protected.

### 6.2 `HD-24` — the blocker was mechanical, and is now removed

With no peer leg, `PosBusDir` is empty → `PosBusReadOthers` returned false → `acctArmed` stayed
false → **ENFORCE was a NO-OP, unreachable in the only configuration that can use it.**
`SoleStrategyOnAccount` (new, default **false**) lets the owner declare that this is the only
strategy on the account, making `others = 0` **known** rather than unknown.

🔴 **The declaration is load-bearing and it is the owner's.** Setting it true while a second
strategy trades the account would let this leg subtract that leg's position as if it were the
human's, and clamp a real exit toward zero. **Default false → undeclared stays BLIND → BLIND
never gates.**
