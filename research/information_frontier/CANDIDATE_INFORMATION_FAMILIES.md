## The two rules this table exists to enforce

> ### **1. `INDICATOR ≠ NEW INFORMATION`.** A product is not a source because its code is
> ### proprietary or its name is impressive. **Map it to observable fields first.**
> ### **2. `RAW OBSERVABLE ≠ INFERRED LATENT`.** Buy the option open interest; ⛔ **never buy the
> ### dealer-gamma inference** — its dealer sign is a free parameter that flips the signal, and
> ### 0DTE, the loudest claim, is the part the data covers worst.
