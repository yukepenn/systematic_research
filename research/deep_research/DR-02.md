## Key mechanisms

- Order-splitting long memory: institutional metaorders are sliced over hours-to-days, making trade-sign autocorrelation a power-law (Hurst ~0.7); continuation lives in ORDER FLOW, but liquidity co-adjusts (asymmetric depth) so PRICES stay near-martingale — the exploitable residual per bar is tiny relative to spread+commission (Lillo/Farmer, https://oms-inet.files.svdcdn.com/production/files/longmemory.pdf; Bouchaud/Farmer/Lillo, https://arxiv.org/abs/0809.0822).
- Square-root impact law: a metaorder of size Q moves price ~ Y·σ·√(Q/V), concave and universal (account-level TSE proof, https://arxiv.org/html/2411.13965v1); impact is partly TRANSIENT — after completion price reverts toward ~2/3 of peak impact (survey https://arxiv.org/pdf/2205.07385). Implication: continuation is expected only WHILE the parent order is still executing; after completion the same mechanism predicts partial mean reversion. A fixed 45-pt NQ move (~0.22% at NQ≈20,000) implies a much larger executed-quantity footprint in a low-vol regime than a high-vol one.
- Kyle/Obizhaeva invariance: bets arrive and are sized per unit of BUSINESS time (trading activity W = σ·P·V; bet size ∝ W^{-2/3}) (Econometrica 2016, https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.3982/ECTA10486). Consequence for us: any fixed point-threshold signal (45 pts, 90/179 SolarWave periods on 1-min clock bars) changes meaning across vol/volume regimes; signals should be evaluated vol-normalized or regime-bucketed, not only in clock time.
- Dealer gamma sign flips the intraday autocorrelation regime: short-gamma dealers hedge WITH the move (amplification/momentum), long-gamma dealers hedge AGAINST it (pinning/reversion); Barbon/Buraschi measure intraday autocorrelation from −63% (positive gamma, illiquid) to positive under negative gamma (https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=3725454); Dim/Eraker/Vilkov confirm with 0DTE-era data (https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/Delivery.cfm/4692190.pdf?abstractid=4692190). Caveat: Cboe finds 0DTE dealer books nearly balanced (https://www.cboe.com/insights/posts/volatility-insights-evaluating-the-market-impact-of-spx-0-dte-options), so the regime exists but is hard to observe without positioning data.
- End-of-day hedging demand: leveraged-ETF rebalancing and option-hedge rebalancing must trade in the SAME direction as the day's move in the final 30-60 minutes, making rest-of-day return predict last-half-hour return in 60+ futures including Nasdaq-100, with reversal over subsequent days — i.e. it is a FLOW effect, not information (Baltussen et al. JFE 2021, https://www3.nd.edu/~zda/intramom.pdf).
- Delayed information incorporation / infrequent rebalancing: agents who rebalance at fixed times (open, close) induce return autocorrelation that switches sign with the rebalancing horizon (Bogousslavsky JF 2016, https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1111/jofi.12436); empirically the Gao et al. first-half-hour→last-half-hour effect is stronger on volatile, high-volume, and macro-news days (JFE 2018) — but Rosa (JFM 2022) shows the unconditional futures version vanished out of sample.
- Stop cascades + liquidity withdrawal: under stress, triggered stops consume depth while HFT liquidity providers withdraw in seconds ('hot potato'), producing self-reinforcing short-horizon continuation until value buyers arrive (SEC/CFTC 2010 report, https://www.sec.gov/files/marketevents-report.pdf; Easley/López de Prado/O'Hara VPIN, https://jpm.pm-research.com/content/37/2/118). Predicts continuation is concentrated in high-vol, one-sided-flow episodes — rare, fat-tailed, and mostly on the short side in equity index futures.
- Session-clock structure: the overnight E-mini drift concentrated at European open (2-3am ET) carried nearly the entire equity premium 1998-2019 (NY Fed SR 917, https://www.newyorkfed.org/research/staff_reports/sr917) but has been ≈0 since 2021 (https://libertystreeteconomics.newyorkfed.org/2026/07/the-disappearing-overnight-drift/): session-time conditioning is real, but signs/magnitudes are unstable across regimes — condition on session, don't bet on a fixed session sign.
- WHEN a 45-pt NQ move should CONTINUE (mechanism synthesis, partly inference): (i) late RTH when aligned with the day's cumulative move (hedging-demand flow); (ii) negative-gamma states (post-selloff, high implied vs realized vol); (iii) high-participation windows (US open, macro releases) where informed/metaorder flow concentrates; (iv) low-vol regimes where 45 pts is a large vol-normalized footprint of an unfinished metaorder; (v) stress/liquidity-withdrawal episodes. WHEN it should REVERT: positive-gamma pinning days; midday low-participation hours; high-vol regimes where 45 pts is within noise; immediately after impact peaks (metaorder completion); end-of-day in the cross-section (single-stock effect, not index).

## What does NOT transfer to 1-min NQ

- Gao et al. (2018) SPY first-half-hour→last-half-hour momentum: estimated on 1993-2013 ETF data; Rosa (JFM 2022, https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1002/fut.22375) tested exactly our instruments (S&P/NASDAQ-100/DJIA futures) out of sample and the predictability DISAPPEARED — cannot be assumed alive in 2023-2026; it must be re-measured in our window before any overlay is built on it.
- Lou/Polk/Skouras overnight-vs-intraday 'tug of war' and Baltussen/Da/Soebhag end-of-day reversal are CROSS-SECTIONAL single-stock effects driven by retail contrarian flow, short-seller risk management, and clientele differences; they net out at the index level by construction and do not exist as stated for NQ futures.
- The overnight drift (2-3am ET) is documented 1998-2019 and has been ≈0 since 2021 (NY Fed 2026) — i.e. it is absent for essentially our whole 2022-2026 research window; do not build a European-open long bias from it.
- The square-root law itself does NOT license '45-pt move → continuation': it is measured on KNOWN metaorders in event time with counterparty labels, is an AVERAGE over the metaorder's life, and implies partial REVERSION after completion. From 1-min OHLCV we cannot observe metaorder boundaries; only the order-splitting sign-autocorrelation transfers, and Lillo/Farmer show liquidity co-adjustment leaves prices near-martingale — expected per-bar drift is likely below NQ spread ($5/tick) + $4.36 RT commission most of the time (Mesfin's MNQ falsification study found gross edges of 0.07-1.50 pts/trade across 14 signal families, all below friction: https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.04004).
- Kyle/Obizhaeva invariance is a cross-asset SCALING law calibrated on portfolio-transition orders — it transfers as a normalization principle (measure moves in vol/business-time units), not as a directional predictor.
- Dealer-gamma regime studies (Barbon/Buraschi; Dim/Eraker/Vilkov) require signed dealer positioning built from option open-interest data (OptionMetrics/CBOE) that we do not have historically for NDX/QQQ; any VIX- or return-based proxy is a weak instrument, and Cboe's evidence that 0DTE books are roughly balanced further weakens proxy validity. A proxy-based null therefore cannot falsify the mechanism itself — only the proxy's usefulness to us.
- Flash-crash stop-cascade evidence is a single-episode, pre-LULD microstructure (stub quotes, 2010 CME logic); current CME velocity-logic/circuit-breaker regimes truncate cascades differently, so 2010 magnitudes do not transfer — only the qualitative asymmetry (stress continuation, mostly short side) does.
- Zarattini/Aziz/Barbon SPY results (and Maróy's optimized Sharpe-3 'improvements') are unreviewed, in-sample-flattering ETF results; the Quantitativo ES/NQ port is practitioner-grade with vol-targeted leverage inflating Sharpe; none of this is evidence for a 1-min NQ edge at our cost structure — at best it motivates the session/noise-band hypotheses we test ourselves.
- Baltussen et al.'s futures intraday momentum reverts over following days — it is a flow effect exploitable only in the final ~30 minutes; it does not support holding trend positions overnight or across sessions, which is consistent with our baseline's exit-on-session-close but rules out 'let it run' extensions justified by that paper.

## Hypotheses

### DR02-H1-TOD-BUCKET — SolarWave baseline profitability is concentrated in high-participation windows: trades ENTERED 08:30-11:30 ET (macro releases + RTH open) and 14:00-16:00 ET (close/hedging window) have strictly higher net expectancy than trades entered 18:00-02:00 ET (Asia) and 11:30-14:00 ET (midday), and at least one of the latter two buckets has expectancy ≤ 0 net of $4.36 RT commission.

**Mechanism.** Metaorder execution, informed flow, and option/leveraged-ETF hedging demand concentrate at the US open and close (Baltussen et al. JFE 2021; Gao et al. JFE 2018; Bogousslavsky JF 2016 rebalancing-time autocorrelation); midday and Asian Globex hours have thin participation where order-splitting continuation is weakest and a trend signal mostly buys noise. The disappearing overnight drift (NY Fed 2026) removes the one documented reason to expect overnight-session alpha in our window.

**Experiment.** Stage A (zero new NT8 runs): in Python (src/analytics/runlib.py outputs), load the canonical baseline trades.parquet (2,915 trades, 2023-01-01→2025-02-02), assign each trade to one of 5 FROZEN entry-time buckets in exchange/ET time: B1 18:00-02:00, B2 02:00-08:30, B3 08:30-11:30, B4 11:30-14:00, B5 14:00-17:00. Compute per-bucket net expectancy ($/trade), PF, and day-level block-bootstrap (5,000 resamples) 90% CIs. Register bucket boundaries in research/registry/hypotheses.md BEFORE running. Stage B (one confirmation NT8 run, only if Stage A passes): version-bumped replica (SolarWaveRKReplicaV0_v2) with an EntryTimeFilter blocking entries in the losing buckets; run over the canonical window with frozen baseline params 90/179/5/10/true/10; ingest via runlib into runs/<run_id>/.

**Pass/fail (preregistered).** Stage A PASS: (B3 ∪ B5) pooled expectancy exceeds (B1 ∪ B4) pooled expectancy by ≥ $15/trade AND B3 and B5 each have bootstrap 90% CI above $0 AND at least one of B1/B4 has point-estimate expectancy ≤ $0. Stage A FAIL: all bucket 90% CIs mutually overlap the pooled mean, or the ordering is absent. Stage B PASS: filtered run retains ≥ 85% of canonical net profit ($124,474+) with ≥ 25% fewer trades and MaxDD no worse than −$22,066.60. All thresholds fixed before execution; no bucket-boundary adjustment permitted after seeing results.

**Priority:** high · **Degrees of freedom:** ~6: five pre-registered bucket boundaries taken from session structure (not tuned) + 1 confirmation run. No parameter search. · **Overfitting risk:** Low-medium. Buckets are frozen ex-ante from exchange-session anatomy, Stage A reuses the immutable canonical run, and there is exactly one confirmation backtest. Main residual risk is post-hoc bucket merging/shifting — explicitly forbidden; any revised bucketing counts as a new hypothesis with a new sequence number.

### DR02-H2-LATE-DAY-MOM — On NQ in our own window (2023-01→2025-02), the rest-of-day return (prior 16:00 ET close → 15:30 ET) positively predicts the last-half-hour return (15:30→16:00 ET): slope b > 0 with Newey-West t ≥ 2.0 and directional hit rate ≥ 53% over ~520 sessions.

**Mechanism.** Gamma and leveraged-ETF hedging demand must trade in the direction of the day's move into the close (Baltussen et al. JFE 2021, mechanism verified on Nasdaq-100 futures 1974-2020); Bogousslavsky-type close-rebalancers add same-direction flow. Rosa (JFM 2022) found the unconditional futures version disappeared out of sample — so this is a genuine, symmetric-risk falsification test of whether the flow channel is alive in the 0DTE era on our exact instrument.

**Experiment.** Pure Python, zero NT8 runs: from the canonical 1-min NQ series (existing merged data; else mcp GetBars), build daily r_ROD = log(P_15:30/P_prev16:00) and r_last30 = log(P_16:00/P_15:30) in ET. Estimate r_last30 = a + b·r_ROD with Newey-West(5 lags); compute sign hit rate; also run the Gao variant (first-half-hour 16:00prev→10:00 predicting last half-hour) as a second frozen spec. Secondary non-gating cut: interaction with VIX≥20 dummy (daily VIX from Cboe public CSV) to check the 'stronger when hedging demand high' prediction. Register both specs before running.

**Pass/fail (preregistered).** PASS: ROD spec has b > 0, NW t ≥ 2.0 AND hit rate ≥ 53%. PARTIAL (recorded, not frontier-entering): only the Gao first-30-min spec passes. FAIL: t < 2.0 or hit rate < 53% on both specs — in which case any close-window continuation overlay on the campaign is rejected and logged in rejected_ideas.md. Criteria frozen before data is touched; no additional predictor windows may be tried under this ID.

**Priority:** high · **Degrees of freedom:** ~2: two literature-frozen specifications, zero tuned parameters, one non-gating interaction term. · **Overfitting risk:** Low. Both specs are fully dictated by published papers; the test is cheap, single-shot, and the published out-of-sample failure (Rosa 2022) means we hold no prior that flatters a pass.

### DR02-H3-VOLSCALED-CONT — Continuation after a fixed-size 45-point NQ directional impulse is a decreasing function of the prevailing volatility regime: in the bottom tercile of trailing 20-day daily-range volatility the mean forward 60-min same-direction drift is ≥ +4 NQ points (net-viable vs ~1.1-pt RT commission + spread), while in the top tercile it is ≤ 0 (reversion).

**Mechanism.** Kyle-Obizhaeva invariance: bets are sized in volatility/business-time units, so a FIXED 45-pt (~0.22%) move in a low-vol regime represents a far larger vol-normalized order-flow footprint — more likely the visible edge of an unfinished metaorder whose order-splitting continuation (Lillo-Farmer long memory) and concave square-root impact still have room to run. In high-vol regimes 45 pts sits inside noise and post-impact transience predicts reversion. This directly answers the packet question of WHEN a 45-pt move continues vs mean-reverts, and simultaneously tests whether SolarWave's fixed point-parameters (90/179/5/10) are regime-fragile.

**Experiment.** Python event study on the canonical 1-min series, spec registered in hypotheses.md before running: event = cumulative move of ≥ 45 pts from a local extremum (extremum = min/max of trailing 30 one-min bars) without an intervening 15-pt retrace; record forward signed returns at +30/+60/+120 min (in move direction); classify each session into terciles by trailing 20-day mean daily high-low range; day-level block bootstrap (5,000 reps) per tercile. Expected N ≥ 1,000 events. If PASS, one confirmation NT8 run: v-bumped replica with entries gated to bottom-two vol terciles (vol state computed from prior 20 sessions, no lookahead) over the canonical window.

**Pass/fail (preregistered).** PASS: bottom-tercile mean forward 60-min continuation ≥ +4.0 pts with bootstrap 90% CI > 0 AND bottom-tercile estimate > top-tercile estimate (which must be ≤ +2 pts). FAIL: bottom-tercile CI includes 0, or ordering reversed/flat. Confirmation-run PASS: vol-gated replica achieves PF ≥ 1.132213 (canonical) with MaxDD improved ≥ 10%. Horizons, thresholds, retrace, swing window, and tercile scheme are frozen ex-ante; only the 60-min horizon gates — 30/120-min are descriptive.

**Priority:** high · **Degrees of freedom:** ~5 frozen construction choices (45-pt threshold inherited from packet/baseline geometry, 15-pt retrace, 30-bar swing window, 20-day vol lookback, terciles) + 1 confirmation run. None searched/tuned. · **Overfitting risk:** Medium. Event-definition choices could be silently re-tuned to manufacture a pass — mitigated by pre-registration and by gating on a single pre-named horizon; the mechanism makes a signed, directional prediction (low-vol continuation, high-vol reversion), so a monotonicity failure is a clean kill.

### DR02-H4-GAMMA-PROXY — Baseline daily PnL is higher on negative-gamma-proxy days than positive-gamma-proxy days: NG-day = (trailing 5-session NQ return < 0 AND prior-day VIX close ≥ its 60-day median); PG-day = (trailing 5-session return > 0 AND VIX < 60-day median); mean(NG) − mean(PG) ≥ $150/day with Welch t ≥ 2.0 and the same sign in both 2023 and 2024.

**Mechanism.** Short-gamma dealer hedging amplifies intraday moves (momentum regime), long-gamma hedging pins price (reversion regime) — Barbon/Buraschi 'Gamma Fragility'; Dim/Eraker/Vilkov 0DTE evidence; Baltussen et al. hedging-demand channel. Dealer books tend toward short gamma after selloffs with elevated implied vol, hence the proxy. A trend-following system like SolarWave should structurally earn more in negative-gamma states.

**Experiment.** Python only: join canonical daily_equity.parquet (daily realized PnL = daily MTM, per campaign conventions) with daily VIX closes (Cboe public historical CSV) and NQ trailing 5-session close-to-close returns over the canonical window (~520 sessions; expected ~100+ NG and ~150 PG days). Compute mean daily PnL by class, Welch t-test, plus per-calendar-year sign check. Proxy definition registered before merging data. No NT8 run unless PASS; then a single confirmation run gating entries on the NG/neutral classes.

**Pass/fail (preregistered).** PASS: mean(NG) − mean(PG) ≥ $150/day, Welch t ≥ 2.0, same sign in 2023 and 2024 sub-samples. FAIL: any criterion missed. Explicitly logged caveat: because the proxy is a weak instrument for true dealer gamma (Cboe finds 0DTE books balanced), FAIL rejects only the PROXY's usefulness, not the mechanism; PASS admits it as a regime filter candidate only, never as standalone evidence of the gamma channel.

**Priority:** medium · **Degrees of freedom:** ~4: 5-session return window, 60-day VIX median lookback, AND-conjunction, $150 threshold — all frozen ex-ante but constructed by us (not literature-standard), which is the highest DoF burn in this packet. · **Overfitting risk:** Medium-high. The proxy is home-made and unobservable ground truth (historical NDX dealer gamma) prevents validation; with ~250 classified days a spurious $150 gap is plausible. Run only after H1-H3 resolve, and treat any pass as provisional pending an orthogonality check against H1's time-of-day and H3's vol-regime effects (the proxy correlates with realized vol by construction).

## Decision

Admit DR02-H1-TOD-BUCKET, DR02-H2-LATE-DAY-MOM, and DR02-H3-VOLSCALED-CONT to the active frontier now; queue DR02-H4-GAMMA-PROXY behind them. Rationale: (1) H1 costs zero new backtests — it re-analyzes the immutable canonical trades.parquet with pre-registered session buckets — and acts directly on the baseline's biggest structural question (whether 24h trading of a trend signal is subsidizing thin-liquidity hours), with a one-run confirmation path that could improve MaxDD without touching frozen parameters. (2) H3 is the packet's core question ('when does a 45-pt move continue?') translated into the best-replicated mechanisms in this literature (invariance scaling + square-root impact + order-splitting memory); it makes a signed, monotone, pre-registered prediction and doubles as a regime-fragility probe of SolarWave's fixed point-parameters. (3) H2 is nearly free and calibrates whether the only well-documented index-futures intraday continuation channel (close-window hedging demand, Baltussen et al. JFE 2021) survived into the 0DTE era on NQ — with Rosa 2022's published out-of-sample failure making it an honest coin-flip rather than a confirmation exercise; either outcome prunes or opens a whole branch (close-window overlays). H4 is deferred because its gamma proxy is a weak, home-made instrument (Cboe evidence of balanced 0DTE books undermines it) and it partially collides with H3's vol conditioning; it runs only after H1/H3 resolve and only as a regime-filter candidate. Overall posture from the source-quality review: mechanisms are real and replicated, but published tradable versions of intraday momentum in index futures have repeatedly died out of sample (Rosa 2022; disappearing overnight drift; Mesfin's all-14-signals-fail MNQ study), so every experiment here is pre-registered, single-shot, and biased toward cheap falsification on our own canonical data before any NT8 confirmation run. Sequence: H1 Stage A and H2 immediately (Python only), H3 event study next, H4 last; all specs and pass/fail thresholds must be committed to research/registry/hypotheses.md before any result is computed, per campaign constitution.
