## Key mechanisms

- SCALE DRIFT IS MECHANICAL, AND ITS SIZE IS PREDICTABLE. For a diffusion, the expected time to a reversal of absolute size delta scales as (delta/sigma)^2 (first-passage scaling), so DC event intensity scales ~(sigma/delta)^2; empirically the exponent is 1.85-1.91 on FX (Glattfelder-Dupuis-Olsen 2011, https://arxiv.org/abs/0809.1040), slightly below 2 because real paths trend. Our 41% selectivity loss (17.8 -> 10.4 vol units) therefore predicts a ~1.71^1.9 ~ 2.7x inflation of flip intensity per unit time in 2025 vs 2023 at fixed 179 ticks — a quantitative, checkable consequence, not a story.
- PERCENT/VOL THRESHOLDS MAKE EVENT STATISTICS STATIONARY BY CONSTRUCTION. The entire DC/intrinsic-time literature (Guillaume et al. 1997; Glattfelder et al. 2011) defines thresholds in RELATIVE units, and the scaling laws holding over ~3 orders of magnitude of percentage thresholds is direct evidence that event counts, overshoot sizes, and durations are stable functions of the relative threshold. Alexander's 1961 filter rule — a percentage trailing-reversal filter structurally identical to Solar Wave's flip rule — is the 60-year-old precedent (https://www.scirp.org/reference/referencespapers?referenceid=3901121).
- THE OVERSHOOT LAW TIES PER-EVENT GROSS TO THE THRESHOLD. On average a DC of size delta is followed by an overshoot of the same magnitude (total move ~ 2*delta; Glattfelder et al. law, overshoot time ~ 2x DC time). Under S_t = k*sigma_t per-event gross scales with sigma_t (stable in vol units), but NQ costs are fixed in dollars ($4.36/RT + slippage in ticks), so proportional scaling drives low-vol-regime trades toward the cost floor — vol normalization stabilizes event geometry but MOVES the cost-fraction problem into low-vol months. This asymmetry is absent from the (frictionless-threshold) DC literature and from daily-rebalance vol-targeting papers.
- NORMALIZATION IS STATIONARITY REPAIR, NOT ALPHA. Fama-Blume 1966 found percentage filters carry no after-cost edge per se; Kim-Tse-Wald 2016 (https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S1386418116301379) show vol-scaling overlays can masquerade as signal improvement; Cederburg et al. 2020 JFE show vol-managed OOS gains are fragile. The literature's honest prediction for S_t = k*sigma_t: event rate and per-event vol-unit economics stabilize (strongly supported), total expectancy does NOT reliably improve (unsupported). Gates must therefore reward per-year stability and plateau width, not total net — which matches the campaign's Wave-1 selection standard.
- VOL IS THE FORECASTABLE INPUT; ESTIMATOR CHOICE MATTERS. Bollerslev-Hood-Huss-Pedersen 2018 RFS (https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article/31/7/2729/5001472) show, on 50+ futures including equity index, that realized-volatility-based (HAR-type) forecasts beat EWMA and rolling windows out-of-sample, and that forecast gains survive costs. Persistence (vol clustering) is what makes a causal sigma_t threshold workable at all; the same persistence means yesterday's RV is already a good 1-day-ahead forecast.
- ESTIMATOR LAG IS PROCYCLICAL IN A SPECIFIC, DANGEROUS DIRECTION. EWMA lambda=0.94 has an 11.2-day half-life; at a vol-regime onset the estimate under-reads for weeks, so a k*sigma threshold is TOO TIGHT exactly when true vol has jumped and slippage is worst — over-triggering whipsaw flips at spike onset — and too wide after vol collapses (missing selectivity). This responsiveness-vs-stability tradeoff is formalized in the margin-model literature (Murphy-Vasios-Vause 2014 BoE, https://www.bankofengland.co.uk/-/media/boe/files/financial-stability-paper/2014/an-investigation-into-the-procyclicality-of-risk-based-initial-margin-models.pdf); rolling windows add the 'ghost effect' (shocks exit the window discontinuously).
- SUB-DAY ESTIMATORS ALIAS THE DIURNAL CYCLE. Andersen-Bollerslev (1997, 1998, 2000; https://public.econ.duke.edu/~boller/Published_Papers/joef_00.pdf) establish that intraday vol is dominated by a deterministic periodic component plus announcement jumps (08:30/10:00/14:00 ET; jumps of 1400-2100% around major releases). A backward-looking intraday ATR window systematically under-estimates sigma right after the 09:30 RTH open and scheduled releases, making a k*sigma threshold too tight precisely at open/news — a mechanism specific to our 1-min frequency that daily vol-targeting papers never encounter. Remedy: freeze sigma at daily scale (prev-session RV) or deseasonalize before any intraday updating.
- FROZEN-AT-EVENT vs CONTINUOUSLY-MOVING (INFERENCE — no direct peer-reviewed evidence found). Continuously recomputing S_t inside an open episode couples the exit distance to the concurrent vol estimate: falling sigma tightens the trail and can trigger 'phantom flips' where the threshold, not the price, moved; rising sigma widens giveback mid-trade. It also destroys the renewal structure that makes DC events well-defined functions of the price path alone. Kaminski-Lo 2014 imply stop value derives from return persistence, not vol tracking, giving no reason to expect within-episode updating to add value. The margin-procyclicality analogy supports freezing at trend birth (H-006's existing design choice).

## What does NOT transfer to 1-min NQ

- Moreira-Muir spanning-regression alphas: derived from monthly-rebalanced factor portfolios via an expected-return-timing channel, shown non-implementable in real time (Cederburg et al. 2020 JFE); our object is an event-definition threshold on one instrument at 1-min, not exposure sizing — do not import the claim that vol management raises expectancy.
- Harvey et al. 2018 Sharpe gains from vol targeting: operate through leverage/exposure choice at daily-portfolio level and the equity leverage effect; Solar Wave is an always-in-market flip strategy with no exposure dial in this design (that dial is the separate, already-registered SW08). Only the tail-reduction logic partially maps.
- FX DC scaling-law EXPONENT VALUES (1.85-1.91): estimated on 2002-2007 24h tick FX with roughly stationary price levels; NQ 2022-2026 has a maintenance-halt session (18:00-17:00 ET), an RTH/ETH two-regime vol structure, strong diurnal periodicity, scheduled macro jumps, and a ~2x price-level trend. The functional form is a strong prior; the numbers are not — they must be re-estimated locally before use in any gate.
- ATR practitioner rules (Turtle 2N stops, ATR-breakout lore): no peer-reviewed support; circulating citations are partly fabricated (see source_quality). Usable as design vocabulary only; carries zero evidential weight for gate-setting.
- Kaminski-Lo stop-loss results: monthly horizon, stop-out into bonds (asset switch); our flip is an immediate reversal, not de-risking, and our persistence structure is at minutes-hours scale. Only the qualitative persistence-dependence transfers.
- BIS/ECB vol-targeting procyclicality (2020 sell-off amplification): a crowding/market-impact externality of institutional AUM flows — irrelevant to a 1-contract historical backtest. Only the estimator-lag mechanics transfer, via the margin-model literature.
- Barroso-Santa-Clara's use of the STRATEGY's own realized vol (6-month window): appropriate for a monthly long-short factor; at our scale the analogous object (realized vol of strategy P&L) is dominated by the threshold recursion's own path noise (documented single-point sensitivity in RE01) and a 6-month window would reintroduce exactly the multi-week lag we flag as dangerous.
- Bloomberg-style intraday vol curve forecasting machinery: needed only if we ever update sigma within the session; with trend-birth-frozen daily sigma (the H-006 design) it is overkill and adds parameters without a hypothesis.

## Hypotheses

### DR4-H1 — On 1-min NQ 2022-01..2026-07, monthly Type-1 flip-event counts under a fixed absolute threshold delta obey log N_m = c + gamma*log(sigma_m/delta) with gamma in [1.5, 2.3] and R^2 >= 0.7 (FX value 1.85-1.91, GRW 2.0), implying the 2025-vs-2023 event-intensity inflation at delta=179 ticks lies in [1.7x, 3.5x]; and a trend-birth-frozen S = k*sigma_prev threshold (k matched to the same total event count) reduces the CV of monthly event counts by >= 40% and drives |corr(N_m, sigma_m)| <= 0.25.

**Mechanism.** First-passage/renewal scaling of reversal events for a near-diffusive path: expected reversal time ~ (delta/sigma)^2, so fixed-tick thresholds have vol-dependent event intensity while k*sigma thresholds decouple intensity from vol by construction (Glattfelder-Dupuis-Olsen 2011 scaling laws; exponent below 2 measures local trendiness — itself a useful diagnostic of whether NQ 1-min paths are more or less trending than FX).

**Experiment.** Pure Python, zero NT8 cost: run the recovered DC engine (src/analytics/solarwave.py) over the existing 737,707-bar ledger for 8 log-spaced fixed deltas (60..400 ticks); count flip events per month; compute sigma_m as monthly mean of daily RV (sqrt of sum of squared 1-min close returns per session); OLS of log N_m on log(sigma_m/delta). Then run solar_wave_adaptive with sigma = 20-session trailing mean of daily RV, frozen at trend birth, k chosen once to match total 2022-2026 event count of delta=179; recompute monthly count CV and corr.

**Pass/fail (preregistered).** PASS requires ALL: (a) gamma-hat in [1.5, 2.3] with R^2 >= 0.7; (b) fixed-179 event-intensity ratio 2025/2023 in [1.7, 3.5]; (c) adaptive monthly-count CV reduction >= 40% vs fixed-179; (d) adaptive |corr(N_m, sigma_m)| <= 0.25. FAIL on any: (a)-(b) failing means NQ 1-min flip statistics are not scaling-law-governed and the k*sigma premise loses its theoretical footing; (c)-(d) failing directly falsifies event-rate stabilization, gutting H-006's rationale before any NT8 spend.

**Priority:** high · **Degrees of freedom:** ~2 effective: sigma estimator fixed in advance (20-session mean of daily RV), k pinned by event-count matching, delta grid mechanical. No P&L enters the criteria. · **Overfitting risk:** Minimal — purely distributional diagnostic on event counts; wide preregistered bands; no economic quantity optimized.

### DR4-H2 — A trend-birth-frozen adaptive threshold S = k*sigma (sigma = trailing 20-session mean of daily RV, no intraday updating; k anchored ONLY by matching the 2023 fixed-179 event rate) run 2022-01..2026-07 achieves, at total trade count within +/-15% of fixed-179: (a) worst-calendar-year slip-1 net >= the fixed-179 worst-year; (b) largest single-year share of total slip-1 net <= 55% (fixed-179 is ~70% in one year); (c) a k-plateau: +/-15% perturbations of k keep slip-1 net within 25% of the center value.

**Mechanism.** Stabilizing delta/sigma stabilizes both event intensity (first-passage scaling) and per-event gross in vol units (DC overshoot law: total move ~ 2*delta), redistributing P&L across vol regimes; per Fama-Blume 1966 and the vol-managed OOS critiques, normalization is stationarity repair rather than alpha, so gates target distributional stability and plateau robustness, not total net — exactly the Wave-1 selection standard.

**Experiment.** New NinjaScript class SolarWaveAdaptiveV1 (fresh class name per hot-reload rule): per-session sigma precomputed in Python from the bar ledger and embedded as a date->threshold-ticks lookup table (removes any NT8 estimator-implementation risk and guarantees Python/NT8 sigma parity); threshold frozen at each trend birth. Run NT8 Strategy Analyzer over the full window, slip 0 and 1 tick, spec.yaml committed BEFORE reading results; k-plateau via 5-point scan {0.70k, 0.85k, k, 1.15k, 1.30k}; ingest via runlib; per-year matched comparisons vs fixed-179; archive per-config daily P&L vectors per protocol.

**Pass/fail (preregistered).** PASS = (a) AND (b) AND (c) simultaneously at trade count within +/-15% of fixed. ### DR4-H3 — Threshold quality in the S = k*sigma family is non-monotonic in estimator lag: with k matched to equal total event count per estimator, both a sub-day estimator (rolling 120-bar intraday ATR) and a 60-session estimator are weakly dominated by 5- and 20-session RV on the pair (worst-year slip-1 net, whipsaw metric = flips/session on top-decile daily-RV days); additionally the sub-day estimator concentrates >= 1.5x the daily-sigma version's event share into 09:30-10:00 ET plus 15-minute windows after 08:30/10:00/14:00 scheduled releases.

**Mechanism.** Two lag pathologies bracket an interior optimum: (i) long-lag estimators (EWMA lambda=0.94 half-life 11.2 days; 60d windows worse) under-read vol at regime onset, making thresholds too tight during spikes when slippage is worst — the procyclicality mechanism of Murphy-Vasios-Vause 2014; (ii) sub-day backward windows alias the deterministic diurnal cycle (Andersen-Bollerslev 1997/2000), under-estimating sigma right after the open and scheduled releases, over-triggering exactly there. Bollerslev et al. 2018 RFS predict RV-based short-window estimators dominate EWMA — but their daily-rebalance setting never tests the sub-day failure mode.

**Experiment.** Python engine ladder, all trend-birth-frozen, over the full ledger: {ATR(120 x 1-min bars), 1d RV, 5d RV, 20d RV, 60d RV, EWMA lambda=0.94 on daily RV}; per-estimator k matched to equal total event count; outputs per estimator: per-year close-fill net, flips/session in top-decile daily-RV days, event-start time-of-day histogram. Only the ladder winner (and 20d as control if different) is promoted to one NT8 confirmation run each under the DR4-H2 harness.

**Pass/fail (preregistered).** PASS if (a) 5d or 20d RV weakly dominates both ATR(120-bar) and 60d on BOTH preregistered coordinates, AND (b) the intraday-ATR variant shows open/news event-share ratio >= 1.5 vs the 20d version. FAIL if the sub-day or 60d estimator strictly wins on both coordinates — which would overturn the lag-danger claim and force a redesign of the sigma prescription in DR4-H2 before promotion. EWMA and 1d RV are reported but ungated (context rungs).

**Priority:** medium · **Degrees of freedom:** The ladder is 6 configs but fully preregistered with ordinal (dominance) criteria and event-count-matched k per rung; ~1 genuine free choice (the whipsaw metric definition, fixed here). · **Overfitting risk:** Moderate: 6 rungs invite winner-picking — contained by allowing promotion only through DR4-H2's independent gates and registering every rung in tested_configs.csv.

### DR4-H4 — Continuously recomputing S_t = k*sigma_t on every bar inside an open episode is dominated by the trend-birth-frozen variant: at identical k and sigma estimator (20-session RV), the continuous version produces >= 10% more flips over 2022-2026 with no improvement in worst-year close-fill net (<= +$0), and its excess flips cluster in top-decile vol-of-vol days (threshold-motion-triggered flips when sigma falls mid-episode; widened giveback when sigma rises).

**Mechanism.** Within-episode updating couples the trailing distance to the concurrent vol estimate, creating a feedback loop analogous to margin procyclicality (Murphy-Vasios-Vause 2014): a falling sigma tightens the trail so the threshold, not the price, triggers the flip ('phantom flips'), while a rising sigma widens giveback mid-trade. It also breaks the renewal property that makes DC events functions of the price path alone (inference — no direct peer-reviewed evidence located; flagged as such). Kaminski-Lo 2014 give no channel by which vol tracking inside a stop adds value absent persistence changes.

**Experiment.** Pure Python A/B on solar_wave_adaptive (both variants are one flag apart in the recovered engine): same sigma series, same k (from DR4-H2 anchoring); over the full ledger compare flip counts, matched-episode P&L, per-year nets, and the timing distribution of flips unique to the continuous variant vs daily-RV-change quantiles. NT8 confirmation run ONLY if the continuous variant wins (upset result), since the frozen variant is already the H-006/DR4-H2 instrument.

**Pass/fail (preregistered).** PASS (frozen design confirmed) if continuous shows >= 10% more flips AND worst-year net improvement <= $0. FAIL if continuous produces < 10% excess flips or improves worst-year net by > $2,500 close-fill — in which case H-006's frozen-at-birth design choice must be reopened before any DR4-H2 promotion.

**Priority:** medium · **Degrees of freedom:** ~1 (the A/B itself; all parameters inherited from DR4-H2). · **Overfitting risk:** Low — a two-arm design comparison with inherited parameters and preregistered ordinal gates; no search.

### DR4-H5 — Pure proportional scaling S = k*sigma pushes low-vol-regime trades onto the fixed dollar cost floor: in the bottom quintile of months by daily RV (2022-2026), the DR4-H2 adaptive configuration has negative aggregate slip-1 net and average per-trade slip-1 net <= 50% of its top-quintile average; consequently a floored rule S = max(k*sigma, delta_min) is required for any promotable variant.

**Mechanism.** The DC overshoot law makes per-event gross proportional to the threshold (total move ~ 2*delta), but NQ costs are constant in dollars ($4.36/RT + tick slippage); as sigma falls, delta = k*sigma shrinks per-event gross toward that floor, so the cost fraction of each trade rises without bound — a friction asymmetry invisible to both the frictionless DC literature and daily vol-targeting papers, and the mirror image of the fixed-tick regime drift the campaign already diagnosed (fixed ticks fail in HIGH-price/vol regimes; pure k*sigma fails in LOW-vol regimes).

**Experiment.** Zero new runs: computed entirely from the DR4-H2 NT8 payloads (trades.parquet + daily RV series) by bucketing months into RV quintiles. If confirmed, the floor remedy enters as a separate, later experiment with delta_min preregistered from cost arithmetic BEFORE any backtest of the floored rule (e.g., delta_min such that k*sigma-implied expected total move 2*delta covers a preregistered multiple of the measured slip-1 all-in round-trip cost), not from a performance scan.

**Pass/fail (preregistered).** PASS (floor required) if bottom-quintile aggregate slip-1 net < $0 AND bottom-quintile avg trade <= 50% of top-quintile avg. FAIL (pure proportional acceptable) if bottom-quintile net >= $0 — in which case delta_min is NOT introduced and the parameter is never burned. The floored rule itself, if triggered, gets its own registered hypothesis with fresh gates.

**Priority:** low · **Degrees of freedom:** 0 at this stage (pure diagnostic on already-produced data); the conditional follow-up would burn 1 (delta_min, but pinned by cost arithmetic, not scanned). · **Overfitting risk:** Low now; the follow-up carries real risk if delta_min were ever tuned on performance — the preregistration explicitly forbids that path.

## Decision

ENTER THE FRONTIER NOW: DR4-H1 and DR4-H2. DR4-H1 costs nothing (pure Python on the existing bar ledger), tests whether the scaling-law machinery that justifies k*sigma thresholds actually governs 1-min NQ, and can kill the whole normalization program for free before any NT8 spend — it should run first and is the cheapest falsification available. DR4-H2 is the sharpened, preregistered version of the already-registered H-006: the literature's clear prediction is that S = k*sigma stabilizes event rate and per-event vol-unit economics (strongly supported by the DC scaling-law literature, which is the direct academic lineage of this strategy class) but does NOT reliably raise total expectancy (Cederburg/Barroso-Detzel OOS and cost critiques; Fama-Blume on percentage filters) — so its gates are deliberately written on worst-year net, concentration share, and k-plateau width rather than total net, matching the campaign's Wave-1 selection standard. DR4-H3 (estimator-lag ladder) enters as the designated follow-up because the packet's key-question answer is: the dangerous lags are BOTH tails — multi-week EWMA/rolling lag (procyclical tightening at spike onset, when slippage is worst) and sub-day windows (diurnal aliasing that over-triggers at the 09:30 open and 08:30/14:00 releases); the literature (Bollerslev et al. 2018) points to 1-20 session realized-vol estimators as the safe interior, and the prescription in DR4-H2 (20-session RV, frozen at trend birth, no intraday updating) is chosen to sit there. DR4-H4 rides along at near-zero cost and defends H-006's frozen-at-birth choice, which currently rests on inference rather than evidence. DR4-H5 is a free diagnostic on DR4-H2's own outputs guarding against the one failure mode vol normalization introduces (cost-floor breach in low-vol regimes); it burns no parameters unless its trigger fires. Not recommended for the frontier: any import of vol-TARGETING position sizing into this packet (that is SW08's separate remit), any ATR-lore-based design shortcuts (evidence base is unreliable to the point of fabricated citations), and any continuously-updated intraday sigma without deseasonalization.
