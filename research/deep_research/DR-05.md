## Source-quality assessment

Three evidence tiers. TIER 1 (peer-reviewed, replicated): (a) DC/overshoot scaling laws — Glattfelder, Dupuis & Olsen 2011, Quantitative Finance (https://arxiv.org/abs/0809.1040), 13 FX pairs, ~3 orders of magnitude, independently replicated and partially extended to CSI300 index futures and Chinese equities (https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0378437116309360); numerics are FX-tick-based, not NQ-1min-based. (b) Market intraday momentum — Gao/Han/Li/Zhou JFE 2018 (https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0304405X18301351) and Baltussen/Da/Lammers/Martens JFE 2021 across 60+ futures incl. equity index (https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0304405X21001598): top-journal, out-of-sample confirmed — this is ANTI-fade evidence and must discipline any failure engine. (c) Reversal after extreme opens in index futures — Grant/Wolf/Yu JBF 2005 (https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0378426604000949), Fung/Mok/Lam JBF 2000 (https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0378426699000722): real but authors themselves show significance 'sharply reduced' by a bid-ask cost proxy; sample ends 2002. (d) Overnight drift and its disappearance — Boyarchenko/Larsen/Whelan, NY Fed SR 917 (https://www.newyorkfed.org/research/staff_reports/sr917) with July-2026 update showing the 2-3am ET window flat over 2021-2025 (https://libertystreeteconomics.newyorkfed.org/2026/07/the-disappearing-overnight-drift/). (e) Gap anomaly — Caporale & Plastun 2017 (https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/10293523.2017.1333563) and Plastun et al. 2020 NAJEF (https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S1062940820300747): mostly efficient; where anomalous (FX), momentum in gap direction, NOT fill. TIER 2 (peer-reviewed mid-tier or SSRN with independent replication): DC-contrarian strategies — Backlash Agent (IEEE CIFEr 2016, https://www.researchgate.net/publication/312498994_Backlash_Agent_A_Trading_Strategy_Based_On_Directional_Change), Intelligent DBA (MDPI Algorithms 2018, https://www.mdpi.com/1999-4893/11/11/171; ~30% annualized in FX AFTER spread but BEFORE full costs), TSFDC (Wiley ISAF 2018, https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/isaf.1425), Adegboye/Kampouridis DC-reversal forecasting (https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0957417421000865); Alpha Engine (Golub/Glattfelder/Olsen 2017, SSRN 2951348, https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=2951348, code public, replications mixed); ORB papers — Holmberg/Lönnbark/Lundström FRL 2013 crude futures (https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S1544612312000438), Zarattini/Barbon/Aziz SSRN 4729284 (https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=4729284) with an independent QuantConnect replication reporting ~17% win rate / ~83% stop-out base rate (https://www.quantconnect.com/research/18444/opening-range-breakout-for-stocks-in-play/); End-of-Day Reversal, Baltussen/Da/Soebhag SSRN 5039009 (https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=5039009). TIER 3 (lore, no peer review, no replication): VWAP-as-attractor claims, Market Profile 70% value area (a rounded 1-sigma convention from Steidlmayer, not an empirical law), practitioner false-breakout playbooks, '70-90% gap fill' statistics. My search found NO peer-reviewed study directly measuring failed-breakout fade profitability in modern index futures — that hole is exactly what our own ledger can fill. Highest-applicability evidence is IN-REPO (user-supplied): SW01 attribution (flip-count chop veto INVERTED: 4+ recent flips = best Type-1 bucket, PF 1.303) and SW02a (last 30 min of holding negative) directly constrain design.

## Key mechanisms

- Overshoot scaling law gives a non-arbitrary failure unit: after a directional change of threshold delta, the AVERAGE overshoot approximately equals delta (law 9 of 12; Glattfelder/Dupuis/Olsen 2011, https://arxiv.org/abs/0809.1040). A Type-1 flip whose overshoot stalls far below theta=179 ticks is therefore statistically anomalous relative to the DC decomposition itself, which lets 'failed persistence' be defined mechanically from our event stream (overshoot deficit) instead of by chart-pattern lore.
- The overshoot distribution is right-skewed (mean ~ delta but median well below it, since a minority of long overshoots carry the mean). DC-contrarian strategies (Backlash/IDBA, https://www.mdpi.com/1999-4894/11/11/171 [MDPI Algorithms 2018]; Alpha Engine, https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=2951348) monetize exactly this: fading at/after DC confirmation wins often and loses occasionally big — the mathematical mirror image of our Solar Type-1 engine, which is why it is the natural complementary return source and why it inherits negative skew that must be capped by hard invalidation.
- Trapped-inventory unwind: breakout entrants and stop-cascade participants hold losing inventory once price re-crosses the breakout level; their exit flow pushes price back toward pre-break value. Academic anchor: intraday reversal after large opening moves in S&P index futures, magnitude increasing in the initial move (Grant/Wolf/Yu 2005, https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0378426604000949; Fung/Mok/Lam 2000, https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0378426699000722). Cost fragility in those papers implies only tightly conditioned events clear modern costs.
- Reversal profit is compensation for liquidity provision and it is ASYMMETRIC: NY Fed shows close-of-day sell imbalances produce robust positive overnight reversals while rally reversals are modest (Boyarchenko/Larsen/Whelan, https://www.newyorkfed.org/research/staff_reports/sr917). Predicts failed DOWNSIDE breaks (long fades back to value) should carry more edge than failed upside breaks — a testable, zero-cost stratification.
- Breakout failure is the base rate, but the continuation side owns the skew: independent replication of the 5-min ORB literature shows ~17% win rate, i.e. ~83% of tight-stop breakouts fail (https://www.quantconnect.com/research/18444/opening-range-breakout-for-stocks-in-play/), yet ORB is net profitable via the fat right tail (Zarattini et al., https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=4729284; Holmberg et al. 2013 crude futures, https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S1544612312000438). A fade engine is structurally short that tail: the trend-resumption exit rule and the Solar-alignment veto are load-bearing, not optional.
- Documented intraday momentum is the opposing force: first half-hour return predicts last half-hour with the SAME sign, strongest on volatile/high-volume/news days (Gao et al. JFE 2018, https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0304405X18301351), partly driven by short-gamma and leveraged-ETF hedging in equity index futures (Baltussen et al. JFE 2021, https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0304405X21001598). Fading is most dangerous precisely on the high-vol days where our SW01 attribution says Solar earns most (high-vol tercile = 58% of net) — the router (fade blocked while trend persists) is literature-aligned economics, not just risk hygiene.
- Gap-fill lore is contradicted by evidence: gap behavior in equities/commodities is mostly consistent with efficiency, and the only robust anomaly found (FX) is continuation IN the gap direction, not fill (Caporale & Plastun 2017, https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/10293523.2017.1333563; Plastun et al. 2020, https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S1062940820300747).
- In-repo constraint that reshapes the whole packet (user data: research/CAMPAIGN_STATE.md, SW01): flip FREQUENCY does not predict failure — 4+ recent flips was the BEST Type-1 bucket (PF 1.303), i.e. flip clusters are range-to-trend transitions, not fade zones. Therefore the failure engine must trigger on the individual event's realized failure (overshoot deficit + re-cross of the flip price) and trade the SECOND leg back to value — never predict failure at flip time from flip counts. Also note the mechanical ordering: re-cross of the old threshold fires ~179 ticks EARLIER than the opposite re-flip, so 'rapid opposite re-flip' is entry-useless (the fade move is already consumed) but is the correct invalidation/exit marker.

## What does NOT transfer to 1-min NQ

- FX scaling-law NUMERICS: OS~delta was calibrated on tick-level FX with percentage thresholds. Our DC is a fixed 179-TICK close-basis filter on an underlying whose price roughly doubled 2022->2026 (in-repo H-006: effective selectivity fell 41%), and 1-min close sampling censors overshoot extremes. The law's functional form may transfer; its constants must be recalibrated per-year on our own ledger before any threshold is used (that is hypothesis DR05-H1).
- Backlash/IDBA/Alpha Engine profitability figures: FX, spread-only cost accounting (IDBA's ~30% p.a. explicitly excludes commissions), and market-making-style inventory scaling that a single-contract NT8 event backtest cannot express. The contrarian-DC mechanism transfers; the returns do not.
- Grant/Wolf/Yu and Fung/Mok/Lam reversal magnitudes: pit-era S&P futures 1987-2002, and the authors show the effect was already cost-marginal THEN with a bid-ask proxy. Two decades of arbitrage and a completely different microstructure sit between that sample and 2022-2026 NQ; only the qualitative trapped-inventory mechanism transfers.
- Overnight-drift timing structure: the 2-3am ET drift window that carried >60% of ES close-to-close return is FLAT over Jan-2021..Dec-2025 (NY Fed Liberty Street, July 2026, https://libertystreeteconomics.newyorkfed.org/2026/07/the-disappearing-overnight-drift/) — i.e. the effect died in exactly our research window. Do not build overnight-clock sleeves from pre-2021 evidence; the thesis's Family-C 'overnight inventory correction' should be deprioritized accordingly.
- Stocks-in-play ORB conditioning: Zarattini/Barbon/Aziz's edge is concentrated by CROSS-SECTIONAL selection (relative volume, news catalysts) across 7,000 stocks. A single-instrument NQ campaign cannot select instruments; only day-type conditioning transfers, and it is a weaker version.
- End-of-Day Reversal (Baltussen/Da/Soebhag): a cross-sectional single-stock long-short driven by retail attention and short-seller risk management; it largely nets out at index level. Only the sign warning about late-day holding transfers — and SW02a already monetized it with the 16:31 timed exit, so no separate sleeve is warranted.
- VWAP-magnet and Market Profile value-area statistics: no peer-reviewed support exists (my searches returned only practitioner guides); the 70% value area is a rounded one-standard-deviation convention, not an empirical law. VWAP/range-midpoint may serve as a bounded TARGET choice inside a failure trade, but 'price is far from VWAP, therefore fade' must not be an entry engine.
- Gap-fill percentage lore ('70-90% of gaps fill'): unsupported; the literature finds efficiency or gap-direction momentum. Any gap sleeve enters only through the cheap preregistered null check DR05-H5, expected to confirm no edge.

## Hypotheses

### DR05-H1_OVERSHOOT_CALIBRATION — On the 2022-01..2026-07 NQ 1-min close-basis DC ledger (theta=179 ticks): (a) the yearly mean overshoot beyond the flip price, measured in flip direction until the opposite flip, lies within [0.5*theta, 1.5*theta] (scaling-law transfer); and (b) 'failed flips' — max overshoot < 0.25*theta (45 ticks) within 60 min of flip confirmation — are followed by a median signed 60-min continuation (flip direction, clocked from the failure stamp) at least 10 ticks WORSE than the unconditional post-flip cohort, with the same sign of difference in >=4 of 5 calendar years.

**Mechanism.** The FX scaling law OS~delta (Glattfelder et al. 2011) supplies the unit; an overshoot deficit signals absence of the persistent order flow a DC presumes, leaving trapped breakout inventory whose unwind pressures price back toward the pre-flip value zone. This is the calibration that converts 'failed breakout' from lore into a measurable event class on OUR stream.

**Experiment.** Pure Python, zero NT8 search budget: extend the existing bar/state ledger (research/01_diagnostics/sw01_bar_ledger.csv via src/analytics/solarwave.py, one exporter-replica rerun if full-window coverage is missing) and compute per flip: overshoot trajectory, time-to-max-OS, first close-basis re-cross of the flip price (>=10-tick margin), signed forward returns at 15/30/60/120 min; stratify year x side x session. Register as instrumentation (seq 0) with spec.yaml committed before reading results.

**Pass/fail (preregistered).** PASS = (a) holds in every calendar year AND (b) failed-flip median 60-min continuation <= unconditional median minus 10 ticks, sign stable in >=4/5 years, pooled Wilcoxon p<0.05. FAIL on either arm kills DR05-H2 unbuilt and is logged in rejected_ideas.md. All three constants (0.25*theta, 60 min, 10 ticks) are frozen here, before any PnL is computed.

**Priority:** high · **Degrees of freedom:** 3 preregistered constants (failure fraction 0.25, window 60 min, re-cross margin 10 ticks); no grid, no optimization, no PnL objective. · **Overfitting risk:** Low — a measurement study with one preregistered cut on already-archived data. Residual risk is silent event re-use for later conditioning; mitigated by logging the events as consumed in the registry.

**Mechanism.** Trapped-trader inventory unwind plus liquidity-provision compensation (DC-contrarian family: Backlash/IDBA/Alpha Engine; index-futures reversal: Grant/Wolf/Yu). Entering on the re-cross trades the SECOND leg after failure is objective — respecting the SW01 inversion (flip frequency predicts continuation, not failure) and capturing the ~179-tick head start over waiting for the opposite re-flip.

**Experiment.** Stage 1: Python vector backtest on the ledger (fills at next 1-min open, slip 0/1/2, full commission), VWAP anchored at the 18:00 ET session open. ## Decision

Decision: admit DR05-H1 to the frontier IMMEDIATELY as instrumentation (zero search-space budget, pure Python on the archived ledger, runnable before SW02 without violating frontier phase gates) — it calibrates the overshoot distribution that every failure definition depends on and pre-freezes all failure constants. Admit DR05-H2 as the campaign's primary complementary-engine candidate, CONDITIONAL on H1(b): it is the only failure definition in this packet that is (i) mechanically precise from our existing DC event stream, (ii) consistent with the SW01 flip-count inversion (it triggers on realized per-event failure, never on flip frequency), and (iii) grounded in Tier-1 evidence (overshoot scaling law + trapped-inventory reversal) rather than lore. Admit DR05-H3 as a sharpening of the already-staged FB01 spec (write the reacceptance clause and n>=150 gate into FB01 now; keep its phase-7 dependency on SW02). DR05-H4 is a free stratified read piggybacking on H2/H3; DR05-H5 is an optional cheap null that permanently closes the gap-fade question. Direct answer to the packet's implementability question — of the three candidate failure definitions from our DC stream: (1) overshoot deficit (max OS < 0.25*theta within 60 min of flip confirmation) is precise and entry-grade; (2) re-cross of the old threshold (first close beyond the flip price by >=10 ticks) is precise and is the correct ENTRY trigger, firing ~179 ticks earlier than any re-flip; (3) rapid opposite re-flip is precise but entry-useless — by confirmation the fade move is already consumed — so it serves as the invalidation/exit marker and post-hoc classifier only. Explicitly REJECTED for the frontier: standalone VWAP-magnet reversion (no evidence base; VWAP enters only as a bounded target choice), overnight-drift timing sleeves (the effect is flat in 2021-2025 — exactly our window, per NY Fed July 2026), unconditioned gap fading (literature-contradicted), and any flip-frequency-triggered fade (inverted by our own SW01 data). Standing caution for all admitted hypotheses: the fade family is structurally short the right tail that funds ORB/trend profits (~83% breakout failure base rate is the opportunity, but the 17% that run are the danger), and JFE-level intraday momentum is strongest on exactly the volatile days where Solar earns most — the trend-resumption forced exit and Solar-alignment veto are load-bearing economics and must never be relaxed to rescue a marginal read.
