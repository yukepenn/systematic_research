## Source-quality assessment

Three distinct evidence tiers apply. TIER 1 (peer-reviewed theorems, independently replicated): change-point optimality — Page (1954, Biometrika), Lorden (1971, Ann. Math. Stat.), Moustakides (1986, Ann. Stat. 14(4):1379-87) proving CUSUM exactly minimizes Lorden's worst-case detection delay at fixed average-run-length-to-false-alarm (survey: https://arxiv.org/pdf/1310.3285, proof exposition: https://inria.hal.science/inria-00072047/document); and small-transaction-cost asymptotics — Whalley-Wilmott (1997), Rogers (2004), Altarovici/Muhle-Karbe/Soner (Finance & Stochastics 2015, https://arxiv.org/abs/1306.2802), synthesized in Muhle-Karbe/Reppen/Soner primer (https://arxiv.org/pdf/1612.01302), whose text I verified directly: proportional cost λ → no-trade band width O(λ^{1/3}), welfare loss O(λ^{2/3}); fixed cost F → band O(F^{1/4}), loss O(F^{1/2}). These are theorems about stylized diffusions, replicated across many author groups. TIER 2 (peer-reviewed theory with thin/mixed empirics): Dixit (JPE 1989, https://www.journals.uchicago.edu/doi/abs/10.1086/261619) hysteresis bands from sunk costs + option value of waiting — theory solid, magnitudes model-dependent; Dai-Zhang-Zhu regime-switching trend following (SIAM J. Fin. Math 2010, https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=1762118; Math of OR 2016, https://pubsonline.informs.org/doi/10.1287/moor.2015.0743) — free-boundary two-threshold structure is rigorous, empirical sections are illustrative daily-frequency backtests without data-snooping control; a 2024 J. Asset Management follow-up exists (https://link.springer.com/article/10.1057/s41260-024-00357-0) but is not a strong independent replication. Glattfelder/Dupuis/Olsen 12 scaling laws (Quantitative Finance 2011, https://www.sg.ethz.ch/publications/2011/glattfelder2011patterns-in-high-frequency/Patterns_in_high_frequency_FX_data_discovery_of_12_empirical_scaling_laws.pdf) — replicated mostly within the Olsen group, FX only. TIER 3 (negative empirics with strong methodology, and practitioner lore): Fama-Blume (J. Business 1966) killed daily filter-rule profits after costs; Sullivan-Timmermann-White (J. Finance 1999, https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1111/0022-1082.00163) showed the best of ~8000 technical rules (incl. all filter rules) do not survive data-snooping adjustment on DJIA/S&P — replicated by Hsu-Kuan (https://homepage.ntu.edu.tw/~ckuan/pdf/snoop01.pdf). Lam-Yam (1997, https://link.springer.com/article/10.1023/A:1009604804110, PDF via ResearchGate) proving filter rules are a special case of CUSUM is a small-journal but structurally correct observation; its performance claims are unreplicated. Sliding-mode chattering/dwell-time results (Liberzon 2003 'Switching in Systems and Control'; survey https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S1367578807000363) are engineering theorems about stability, not profitability — analogy value only. CRITICAL GAP: no paper gives a universal exit-to-reversal threshold RATIO; the free-boundary literature makes the gap an output of cost/vol/regime-persistence, not a constant. Anything below phrased as a specific ratio is inference, flagged as such.

## Key mechanisms

- STRUCTURAL IDENTIFICATION (highest-value finding): the Solar Wave flip rule — reverse on an S-tick retracement of close from the running close-extreme 'anchor' — is EXACTLY a one-sided CUSUM on price changes with reference value k=0 and threshold h=S, because CUSUM S_t=max(0,S_{t-1}−r_t) equals drawdown-from-running-max. Lam-Yam (1997) prove filter/trailing rules are this special case (https://link.springer.com/article/10.1023/A:1009604804110). Consequence: the entire CUSUM design theory applies to our exact baseline with zero reinterpretation.
- CUSUM design theory (Page 1954; Moustakides 1986, Ann. Stat.): to detect a drift change from +δ to −δ with minimal worst-case delay, the optimal reference value is k=half the total drift shift, NOT zero; with k>0 the false-alarm run length grows EXPONENTIALLY in threshold h (Siegmund: ARL0 ≈ [e^{2k(h+1.166)} − 2k(h+1.166) − 1]/(2k²), https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/03610920500440032, https://www.eng.auburn.edu/~maghssa/INSY7330/Cusum-Control-Charts-M2013-Maghsoodloo.pdf) while detection delay grows only LINEARLY (≈h/(δ−k)). At k=0 (our baseline) false-alarm suppression is only polynomial in h. This asymmetry — exponential benefit vs linear cost of raising the reversal threshold — exists only when k>0, and is the principled answer to 'how should the reversal-confirmation threshold relate to the exit threshold'. [Inference applying theorem to our setting: adding a per-bar drift allowance k>0 is a structurally new knob that no fixed-tick retracement distance can replicate, because a slow adverse grind below k/bar never accumulates.]
- Real-options hysteresis (Dixit, JPE 1989, https://www.journals.uchicago.edu/doi/abs/10.1086/261619): with any sunk switching cost and uncertainty, optimal entry and exit triggers strictly straddle the Marshallian break-even, and the band is 'significant even with small sunk costs' because the option value of waiting adds to the direct cost. Trading translation: the costlier action (full reversal = round-trip + new exposure = 2 position units) rationally requires strictly more evidence than the cheaper action (going flat = 1 unit), i.e. S_reverse > S_exit, with a flat state in between.
- Quantitative band-width scaling (verified in Muhle-Karbe/Reppen/Soner primer text, https://arxiv.org/pdf/1612.01302): proportional cost λ → no-trade band width O(λ^{1/3}) with welfare loss O(λ^{2/3}); fixed per-trade cost F → band width O(F^{1/4}) with loss O(F^{1/2}) (Altarovici et al, https://arxiv.org/abs/1306.2802). The mechanism is generic — suboptimality cost of deviation is quadratic, trading cost linear/constant, so the optimum balances them at a fractional power (https://arxiv.org/html/2603.29994v1). Our per-flip cost is fixed per contract (≈$4.36 commission + ~2 ticks spread ≈ $14.4/RT), so the F^{1/4} regime applies [inference]: the optimal hysteresis gap has very LOW cost-elasticity — doubling costs justifies only ~19% more band — so cost alone cannot justify a large S_reverse/S_exit ratio; any large optimal gap must be signal-driven, and this is directly testable.
- Regime-switching optimal trend following (Dai-Zhang-Zhu, SIAM J. Fin. Math. 2010, https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=1762118; Math OR 2016, https://pubsonline.informs.org/doi/10.1287/moor.2015.0743): the optimal rule under a hidden bull/bear Markov chain is two thresholds on the posterior bull probability, p_sell < p_buy, with an INACTION (flat) region between them; the gap is a free-boundary output that widens with transaction cost and noise and narrows with regime persistence. The flat region's cost is forgone in-regime drift times occupancy; it is optimal precisely when the posterior is too ambiguous to cover a round-trip. No closed-form ratio exists — the literature's honest answer to the packet question is 'the ratio is an output, not an input'.
- Chattering and dwell time (sliding-mode control surveys, https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S1367578807000363; Liberzon 2003): a relay with a single threshold under noise switches at unbounded frequency (chattering/Zeno); the two standard fixes are a hysteresis band wider than the noise amplitude and a minimum dwell time between switches. These guarantee bounded switching and stability, NOT profit — the analogy motivates the state-machine architecture (two thresholds + optional refractory period) but carries zero information about expectancy.
- Directional-change overshoot law (Glattfelder-Dupuis-Olsen 2011, https://www.sg.ethz.ch/publications/2011/glattfelder2011patterns-in-high-frequency/Patterns_in_high_frequency_FX_data_discovery_of_12_empirical_scaling_laws.pdf): after a δ-threshold directional change, the average continuation (overshoot) is ≈δ itself, stable over ~3 orders of magnitude in FX. This is the strongest empirical argument AGAINST a very wide reversal-confirmation threshold: if the expected post-confirmation continuation is only ≈δ, demanding 2δ of confirmation spends most of the expected move buying certainty [transfer to NQ unverified — see not_transferable].
- Negative empirical prior (Fama-Blume 1966; Sullivan-Timmermann-White, J. Finance 1999, https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1111/0022-1082.00163): daily filter rules die after costs, and the best-of-universe technical rule on DJIA does not survive White's reality-check bootstrap. The methodological transfer is the important part: any (S_exit, S_reverse) grid search we run must be judged against a snooping-adjusted benchmark (our CPCV/neighbourhood-plateau gates), not raw best-cell performance.

## What does NOT transfer to 1-min NQ

- Small-cost asymptotic band formulas (Whalley-Wilmott λ^{1/3}, Altarovici F^{1/4}) assume a continuously divisible position, CRRA utility, and geometric Brownian dynamics; we trade exactly ±1 NQ contract, all-or-nothing, on a PnL objective with heavy-tailed 1-min returns. Only the scaling EXPONENT and the existence of an inaction band transfer as priors; the constants and the 'trade back to band edge' policy do not.
- CUSUM optimality (Moustakides) and Siegmund ARL formulas assume i.i.d. observations with known pre/post-change distributions. 1-min NQ has a strong intraday volatility U-shape, vol clustering, and session breaks, so Gaussian ARL calibration of absolute thresholds will be wrong by large factors. Usable only for RELATIVE design (shape of k-h tradeoff, matched-trade-count comparisons) or after per-bar volatility standardization — which interacts with registered H-006 and must not be double-counted as two independent confirmations.
- Dai-Zhang-Zhu threshold VALUES are calibrated to daily S&P data with bull/bear regime durations of months-to-years and an infinite discounted horizon. Our episodes are intraday-to-multiday and force-flattened at every session close (strategy is flat at 17:00 ET), which censors exactly the long-duration states their solution exploits. Only the two-thresholds-plus-inaction-region STRUCTURE transfers.
- Glattfelder overshoot≈δ scaling is from 24h FX with sub-pip spreads and no session boundary; NQ session breaks and the exit-on-close architecture truncate overshoots, and equity-index microstructure differs. The 'don't over-confirm' argument is a prior to test, not a fact about NQ.
- Fama-Blume/STW negative results concern DAILY filter rules on pre-decimalization equities; they do not directly indict intraday futures trailing rules (our SW01b already rejected the pure-drift null at p=0.0323). What transfers is the data-snooping discipline, not the verdict.
- Sliding-mode dwell-time guarantees concern actuator stability. Worse, our own SW01 attribution INVERTED the chop-veto: 4+ flip episodes are the BEST bucket (PF 1.303), so whipsaw-suppression mechanisms imported from control theory have an adverse prior in this specific strategy and must be treated as falsification targets, not fixes.
- The 'cost of the flat state' in regime-switching models is symmetric forgone drift. NQ 2023-25 has strong positive drift and our SW01b showed exit machinery + drift alone yields median $58.6k; a flat state therefore costs asymmetrically more on the long side in this sample, and any flat-state experiment must use exposure-normalized and per-side gates or it will just re-measure drift.

## Hypotheses

### DR03-H1-SPLIT-THRESHOLDS — In SolarWaveOpenV1, splitting the single distance S=179 into an exit distance S_x and a strictly larger reversal distance S_r (exit current position at S_x-tick retracement from anchor; enter the opposite side only at S_r-tick retracement, flat in between) yields a configuration off the diagonal S_r=S_x that beats the best diagonal configuration on after-cost PF and MAR at >=85% of baseline net — i.e., the Dixit asymmetry (costlier action needs more evidence) is real here and the flat state's forgone-drift cost is smaller than the whipsaw cost it removes.

**Mechanism.** Dixit (1989) hysteresis: reversal costs 2 position units + round-trip friction vs 1 unit for exit, so optimal triggers straddle; Dai-Zhang-Zhu free-boundary structure says an inaction region is optimal when the signal is ambiguous; fixed-cost band asymptotics (F^{1/4}) bound how much of any observed gap can be cost-justified. This is the theory-grounded completion of already-registered H-007, now reachable because RE01 recovered the math.

**Experiment.** New NinjaScript class SolarWaveOpen_HystV1 (fresh class name per hot-reload convention) with parameters (S_x, S_r), S_r>=S_x, flat state between; anchor logic unchanged, strict inequality preserved. Stage 1: Python close-fill screen via src/analytics/solarwave.py ledger replay to prune the grid. Stage 2: NT8 Strategy Analyzer runs on canonical window 2023-01-01T06:00Z→2025-02-02T22:59:59Z, Lifetime commission, 0- and 1-tick slippage, spec.yaml committed to runs/<run_id>/ BEFORE reading results. Preregistered grid: S_x ∈ {130,179,230}; ratio r=S_r/S_x ∈ {1.0,1.15,1.3,1.5,2.0} (15 cells; r=1.0 column = pure-reversal control). Also record per-cell: trade count, flat-state occupancy %, counterfactual PnL of flat intervals (cost-of-flat-state measurement), per-side PF.

**Pass/fail (preregistered).** PASS iff at 1-tick slippage: (a) some cell with r>1 has PF >= best r=1 cell PF + 0.02 AND MAR (net/maxDD) >= 1.15x best r=1 cell, with net >= 85% of baseline $146,440; (b) the off-diagonal>=diagonal ordering holds separately in 2023 and in 2024 (sign consistency); (c) at least two r-adjacent cells satisfy (a)'s direction (plateau, no single-cell spike). Mitigations: coarse preregistered grid, sub-year sign-consistency gate, plateau requirement, canonical window only (no 2022, no locked-forward), and the falsification branch (diagonal optimum) is a fully acceptable, registerable outcome.

### DR03-H2-CUSUM-DRIFT-ALLOWANCE — Replacing the flip statistic 'retracement from close-extreme' (= CUSUM with reference value k=0) with a true CUSUM carrying per-bar drift allowance k>0 (S_t = max(0, S_{t-1} + (−r_t) − k), flip when S_t > h, h re-solved to match baseline trade count ±10%) improves matched-frequency after-cost net by >=10% and worst-year net, because k>0 converts false-alarm suppression from polynomial-in-h to exponential-in-h and immunizes the statistic against slow adverse grinds.

**Mechanism.** Lam-Yam (1997): filter rule ≡ CUSUM(k=0). Page/Moustakides: optimal k equals half the drift shift between up- and down-trend states; Siegmund ARL: with k>0 false alarms fall exponentially in h while detection delay grows only linearly — the only mathematically principled way to buy fewer false reversals cheaply. k=0 (baseline) is the maximally noise-sensitive member of the family and is nested, so the test is a clean one-parameter extension.

**Experiment.** Stage 1: Python screen in src/analytics/solarwave.py (function alongside solar_wave_adaptive) implementing the k-CUSUM ladder on the 737k-bar sw01 ledger; k grid {0.5,1,2,4} ticks/bar plus k=0 control; for each k solve h by bisection to match baseline trade count ±10% (h is slaved, not free). Stage 2: NinjaScript class SolarWaveOpen_CusumV1 for the surviving k values; NT8 canonical-window runs, Lifetime commission, 1-tick slippage, immutable runs/<run_id>/ with preregistered spec.yaml. Report per-year net, PF, DD, and flip-latency distribution vs baseline.

**Pass/fail (preregistered).** PASS iff some k>0 at matched trade count achieves: net >= 110% of the k=0 matched control at 1-tick slippage AND worst-year (2023/2024/partial-2025) net strictly improves AND PF >= baseline 1.1322. FAIL iff net is monotonically non-increasing in k across the grid, or the best k>0 cell's improvement disappears at 1-tick slippage. Secondary preregistered readout (not a gate): whether improvements concentrate in low-path-efficiency episodes identified by SW01 (eff_120<=0.035 quartile).

**Priority:** high · **Degrees of freedom:** 1 new structural parameter (k); 4 non-zero values; h consumed by trade-count matching so it burns no independent freedom · **Overfitting risk:** Medium-low. The family nests baseline, h is slaved to frequency matching, the grid is 4 points, and the theory prediction (k = half drift shift, i.e. order 0.5-2 ticks/bar given NQ per-bar drift) was stated before any result. Interaction risk with H-006 (vol scaling) must be handled by testing k on RAW ticks first, vol-normalized variants only as a follow-up registry entry.

### DR03-H3-DWELL-TIME-FALSIFICATION — A minimum dwell time τ>0 after each flip (suppress further flips for τ minutes; first violation after τ executes) does NOT improve the baseline: no τ ∈ {5,15,30,60} min achieves net >= 100% of baseline with maxDD <= 90% of baseline in both 2023 and 2024 — i.e., the control-theory chattering fix fails in this strategy, as predicted by SW01's inverted chop-veto (4+ flip episodes are the best bucket, PF 1.303).

**Mechanism.** Dwell-time switching (Liberzon 2003) bounds switching frequency and guarantees stability in control systems; the trading analogue is a refractory period. But SW01 attribution showed flip clusters carry disproportionate profit here, so the campaign's prior is that dwell time deletes profitable re-flips. Running this as a preregistered FALSIFICATION retires a perennial practitioner temptation ('minimum hold time') cheaply and permanently, and if it unexpectedly passes, that overturns the SW01 interpretation and is even more informative.

**Experiment.** NinjaScript class SolarWaveOpen_DwellV1 with parameter τ ∈ {5,15,30,60} minutes; anchor updating continues during the refractory window; deferred flip executes on the first qualifying bar after τ. Four NT8 canonical-window runs + baseline control, Lifetime commission, 1-tick slippage, immutable run dirs with preregistered spec.yaml. Python post-processing joins to the SW01 episode ledger to attribute which flips were suppressed and their counterfactual PnL.

**Pass/fail (preregistered).** The registered hypothesis is the NEGATIVE. It PASSES (dwell time rejected) iff no τ cell reaches net >= 100% baseline AND maxDD <= 90% baseline in both sub-years. It FAILS (control-theory fix vindicated, SW01 interpretation must be revisited) iff any τ cell meets both gates in both sub-years — that outcome forces a new registry entry re-examining the flip-cluster attribution before any adoption.

**Priority:** medium · **Degrees of freedom:** 1 parameter, 4 values; zero adoption intent — this is an idea-retirement experiment · **Overfitting risk:** Low. Negative preregistration with adverse prior; either outcome is recorded in rejected_ideas.md or triggers mandatory re-attribution; nothing is tuned.

### DR03-H4-COST-ELASTICITY-OF-BAND — The PF-maximizing single reversal distance S* is cost-elastic with log-log slope in [0.15, 0.45] against per-round-trip cost c (bracketing the theoretical F^{1/4}=0.25 fixed-cost exponent and λ^{1/3}≈0.33 proportional exponent): elasticity(log S*, log c) ∈ [0.15,0.45] and S* monotone non-decreasing in c over c ∈ {$5, $14.36, $30, $60}. If instead the slope is <=0.05, the optimal band is cost-insensitive (signal-driven), which caps the expected benefit of H1's hysteresis gap and reprioritizes it.

**Mechanism.** Transaction-cost asymptotics (verified in https://arxiv.org/pdf/1612.01302): inaction-band width scales as F^{1/4} for fixed per-trade costs, λ^{1/3} for proportional — because deviation loss is locally quadratic while trading cost is linear/constant. Measuring the actual elasticity on our data tests whether the diffusive mechanism operates at 1-min NQ scale at all, and calibrates BEFORE H1 how much gap the $14.36/RT friction can justify (prediction: little — doubling cost justifies only ~19% wider band).

**Experiment.** Pure Python, zero new strategy code, zero NT8 runs: replay the ledger via src/analytics/solarwave.py over the SW01 ledger window; for each per-RT cost c ∈ {$0, $5, $14.36, $30, $60} compute net over S grid {90,105,...,300} (15 points); S*(c) = argmax net; OLS of log S* on log c over the four c>0 points; bootstrap the slope CI by yearly block resampling. Results filed under runs/<run_id>/ with spec.yaml preregistered like any experiment; close-fill approximation documented as a known limitation.

**Pass/fail (preregistered).** PASS iff slope ∈ [0.15, 0.45] AND S*(c) monotone non-decreasing. FAIL-A (cost-insensitive band) iff slope <= 0.05 or non-monotone: record that hysteresis-gap sizing cannot be justified by cost arguments on this data, downgrade H1's expected effect size in its spec BEFORE H1 stage-2 runs. FAIL-B (super-elastic, slope > 0.6): flags that the close-fill screen is distorting optima and NT8 verification is mandatory before using any Python-screen argmax anywhere in Wave 2.

**Priority:** medium · **Degrees of freedom:** 0 new strategy parameters (cost is a simulation input; S grid reuses the known family); interpretation-only experiment · **Overfitting risk:** Low. Nothing is adopted; it is a mechanism discriminator whose main function is to discipline H1's expected effect size and to stress-test the Python screen against NT8 ground truth.

## Decision

Enter DR03-H1 and DR03-H2 into the active frontier; run DR03-H4 first as a cheap gate-setter; hold DR03-H3 as an opportunistic idea-retirement run. Rationale: (1) The packet's central discovery is structural, not empirical — the recovered Solar Wave flip rule IS a k=0 CUSUM (Lam-Yam), which means Moustakides/Page/Siegmund theory applies to our exact baseline: H2's drift-allowance k is the single most theory-privileged new knob available to the campaign (exponential false-alarm suppression vs linear delay cost exists only at k>0), it nests the baseline, and it burns one degree of freedom. (2) H1 is the rigorous completion of already-registered H-007 with the Dixit/Dai-Zhang-Zhu asymmetry prior (reverse requires more evidence than exit) and a preregistered 15-cell grid with plateau and sub-year-consistency gates; its falsification branch (optimum on the diagonal) is exactly H-007's registered kill condition, so it cannot dangle. (3) H4 costs one Python afternoon and zero NT8 credibility: the fixed-cost F^{1/4} scaling predicts our $14.4/RT friction justifies only a small hysteresis gap, so measuring the band's actual cost-elasticity BEFORE H1's NT8 stage prevents us from over-interpreting any large off-diagonal winner as cost-rational when it would actually be a signal artifact. (4) H3 is deliberately registered as a negative: the sliding-mode dwell-time analogy is the most seductive import from control theory, and SW01's inverted chop-veto (flip-rich episodes are the best bucket) predicts it fails here; a preregistered rejection closes that door permanently for the cost of four runs. On the packet's two headline questions the literature's honest answers are: the exit/reversal threshold ratio is a free-boundary OUTPUT depending on cost, noise, and regime persistence — no universal constant exists, and fixed-cost asymptotics cap the cost-justified gap at a weak F^{1/4} elasticity; and the cost of the flat intermediate state is forgone in-regime drift times occupancy, which on drift-heavy 2023-25 NQ is asymmetric against the long side — hence H1's mandatory exposure-normalized, per-side, and counterfactual-flat-PnL readouts. Sequencing note: H2 must be tested on raw ticks before any vol-normalized variant to avoid double-counting with registered H-006, and every experiment respects the campaign's immutable-run/preregistered-spec discipline with data-snooping-aware gates (the STW lesson), canonical window only.
