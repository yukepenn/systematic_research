## Key mechanisms

- False Strategy Theorem / extreme-value mechanism: the expected maximum Sharpe ratio of N independent zero-skill trials grows like sqrt(2 ln N) times the cross-trial SR dispersion, so the best of a family looks skilled by construction; MinBTL inverts this to give the minimum sample length needed to distinguish the max from noise (Bailey-López de Prado DSR, https://www.davidhbailey.com/dhbpapers/deflated-sharpe.pdf; Pseudo-Mathematics, https://www.ams.org/notices/201405/rnoti-p458.pdf). Our 90 trials on ~4.6y are far fewer EFFECTIVE trials because dense 1-D scans are highly correlated — deflation must use clustered N_eff with brackets, not raw N.
- PBO/CSCV mechanism: partition the T×N daily P&L matrix into S equal time blocks, form all C(S,S/2) symmetric IS/OOS recombinations, and for each record the OOS relative rank of the IS-best config; PBO = fraction of splits where the IS winner falls below OOS median. It measures the stability of the SELECTION PROCEDURE, not the truth of any one config, and consumes exactly the artifact this campaign already archives (per-config daily P&L vectors) (Bailey-Borwein-López de Prado-Zhu, https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=2326253). Known failure modes: blind to leakage/lookahead, and dilutable by padding the trial set — the trial registry must be frozen to ALL logged configs.
- PSR mechanism: the sampling variance of the Sharpe estimator is inflated by negative skew and excess kurtosis (Mertens expansion); PSR = P(true SR > SR0) given T, skew, kurt. Critically, positively skewed trend-following P&L (like Solar's) gets a LOWER SR standard error, i.e. skew-aware testing is more favorable than naive t-tests here — this cuts in our favor and should be computed from empirical moments, not assumed Gaussian (Bailey-López de Prado, https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=2460551).
- DSR mechanism: DSR = PSR evaluated at SR0 = expected max null Sharpe given the number and dispersion of trials actually run, with the effective trial count estimated by clustering the trial correlation matrix (Deflated Sharpe Ratio, https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=2460551; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deflated_Sharpe_ratio). For a dense 1-D StopMultiplier scan whose neighbors share >90% of trades, N_eff is plausibly 5-15, not 90 — but unlogged 'mental trials' (design decisions, inverted vetoes) push the other way, hence bracketing N in {N_eff_clustered, N_raw, 3×N_raw} as already adopted in campaign protocol.
- White Reality Check mechanism: to test 'does the BEST of the family beat a benchmark', bootstrap the joint T×N matrix of performance differentials with the stationary bootstrap and compare the observed max statistic to the bootstrapped max distribution — the max is taken inside every bootstrap replication, so the entire search is priced in (White 2000 via Hsu-Kuan, https://homepage.ntu.edu.tw/~ckuan/pdf/snoop01.pdf).
- Hansen SPA mechanism: the Reality Check is conservative under the least-favorable configuration and loses power when many bad configs are in the family (our dense scans include known-bad tails); SPA fixes this by studentizing each config's statistic and recentering deeply-negative configs so junk trials no longer dilute the test (Hansen 2005 via Hsu-Kuan, https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=685361). This makes SPA the correct family-level test for a registry that honestly logs bad configs.
- Romano-Wolf StepM mechanism: stepwise max-statistic testing identifies WHICH configs beat the benchmark with familywise-error control, capturing the joint dependence of the 90 P&L streams and gaining power over single-step methods with each rejection round (Romano-Wolf 2005, https://statweb.stanford.edu/~romano/fwe2.pdf). It converts 'the family has an effect' into a defensible surviving SET — exactly what plateau-based promotion needs.
- Harvey-Liu haircut mechanism: convert the strategy t-stat to a multiple-testing-adjusted p-value (Bonferroni/Holm/BHY over an assumed trial count) and back to a haircut Sharpe; the haircut is nonlinear — high-SR strategies are moderately penalized, marginal ones destroyed (Harvey-Liu Backtesting, https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=2345489; code https://people.duke.edu/~charvey/backtesting/). With Solar's full-history PF of 1.06, we are in the heavily-penalized marginal zone — this is the binding gate, and the t>3.0 hurdle of Harvey-Liu-Zhu (https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article/29/1/5/1843824) is the right order of magnitude for a mined family.
- Stationary bootstrap mechanism: resampling geometric-random-length blocks of the DAILY P&L vector preserves serial dependence and vol clustering that IID resampling destroys; block length chosen by the corrected Politis-White automatic rule (https://mathweb.ucsd.edu/~politis/SBblock-revER.pdf; correction https://public.econ.duke.edu/~ap172/Patton_Politis_White_2009.pdf). For trend-following P&L, losses cluster in chop regimes, so IID trade-shuffling systematically understates tail drawdown (inference supported by practitioner demonstrations, e.g. https://www.newsletter.quantreo.com/p/max-drawdown-is-a-distribution-not).
- Our strategy is flat at every session close and no trade spans a roll gap (frozen truth), so the daily P&L vector is a complete sufficient statistic for the strategy path — bootstrap and CSCV blocks defined on days/months have NO within-trade leakage. This makes the campaign unusually well-suited to matrix-based methods; the only cross-block state is indicator warm-up, handled by the already-preregistered per-fold warm-up. (Inference from campaign structure + purged-CV logic, https://www.risklab.ai/research/backtesting/backtesting_cross_validation.)
- Breiman instability mechanism — the direct answer to ±$40-90k single-point chaos: argmax selection on a discontinuous, chaotic objective is an 'unstable procedure' (small data perturbation → different selected model → large predictive loss), and unstable procedures are improved by PERTURB-AND-AVERAGE: evaluate a smoothed functional (neighborhood/trimmed-mean of the objective) or aggregate over perturbed selections rather than selecting the pointwise max (Breiman 1996, https://projecteuclid.org/journals/annals-of-statistics/volume-24/issue-6/Heuristics-of-instability-and-stabilization-in-model-selection/10.1214/aos/1032181158.full). Peer-reviewed plateau-search work applies exactly this to trading parameters (https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S095070512400265X).
- Why the parameter map is chaotic (mechanism, not mystery): a directional-change threshold recursion re-partitions the ENTIRE price path into a different event sequence when the threshold crosses any historical extremum-to-extremum span; the number of DC events scales as a power law in the threshold, and one added/removed flip propagates through the one-state recursion forever (Glattfelder-Dupuis-Olsen, https://arxiv.org/pdf/0809.1040; matches RE01's recovered close-basis DC filter). Therefore pointwise net-P&L(θ) is a high-variance, discontinuous estimator of the neighborhood-mean economics, and only neighborhood functionals are statistically meaningful selection targets (inference from RE01 + DC literature).
- Multi-path vs single-path validation: single-path walk-forward tests one arbitrary chronology and shows higher false-discovery rates and temporal variability than combinatorial multi-path methods (CPCV/CSCV) in the only controlled synthetic comparison to date (Arian-Norouzi-Seco 2024, https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0950705124011110). This supports the campaign's existing decision to replace 18/6 WFO with CPCV over monthly blocks.

## What does NOT transfer to 1-min NQ

- Harvey-Liu-Zhu's numerical calibration (t>3.0, the 316-factor zoo, their correlation structure) is fitted to cross-sectional equity factor publications spanning decades; our setting is a within-family parameter scan on one instrument. The FRAMEWORK (haircuts under bracketed N) transfers; the specific hurdle numbers do not — we must supply our own trial counts and correlation structure (https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article/29/1/5/1843824).
- Empirical conclusions of the RC/SPA technical-analysis literature (rules profitable pre-1990s, dead after; Hsu-Kuan, Park-Irwin) were derived on daily bars, mostly cost-free, on equities/FX/older futures. None of it predicts anything about 1-min NQ 2022-2026 with $4.36/RT + slippage; only the testing machinery transfers (https://homepage.ntu.edu.tw/~ckuan/pdf/snoop01.pdf).
- MinBTL and the raw False-Strategy-Theorem E[max SR] assume INDEPENDENT trials; applying them with N=90 to a dense scan where adjacent configs share >90% of trades wildly overstates the required backtest length and the deflation benchmark. Use clustered N_eff with brackets instead (https://www.ams.org/notices/201405/rnoti-p458.pdf).
- CPCV's purging/embargo machinery was designed for ML labels that span many bars (feature-label overlap leakage). Our strategy is flat at session close with no overlapping labels, so heavy purging is unnecessary; the only transferable piece is per-fold indicator warm-up (recursion state), which the campaign already preregistered. Mechanically importing embargo windows would just burn data (https://www.risklab.ai/research/backtesting/backtesting_cross_validation).
- Pardo-style walk-forward-as-deployment (periodic re-optimization, WFE>0.7 acceptance) presumes a live re-fitting workflow; this campaign is historical-research-only with a frozen-parameter thesis, and the WFE thresholds are uncalibrated lore anyway (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Walk_forward_optimization).
- Bootstrap-the-PRICES Monte Carlo (synthetic paths, GAN/Boltzmann resampling of bars) is invalid for our threshold recursion: resampled price paths break the recursion's state continuity and the DC event partition, producing P&L from a strategy that never ran. Bootstrap must operate on realized P&L vectors (as RC/SPA/PBO do), never on bars fed back through the strategy (inference from RE01 recursion structure; consistent with DC scaling behavior, https://arxiv.org/pdf/0809.1040).
- IID trade-shuffling Monte Carlo (ubiquitous in NT8/retail tooling) assumes exchangeable trades; with regime-clustered losses it understates tail drawdown and cannot be used for risk gates, only as a lower-bound sanity check (practitioner demonstration, https://www.newsletter.quantreo.com/p/max-drawdown-is-a-distribution-not; no peer-reviewed defense of the IID variant exists).
- CSCV's implicit stationarity-across-blocks premise is weakened in our data: H-006 shows effective selectivity fell 41% from 2023→2025 (fixed-tick threshold vs growing NQ price), so IS/OOS block recombinations mix structurally different regimes. PBO remains valid as a RELATIVE diagnostic of selection stability, but a low PBO here cannot certify forward viability of a config whose selectivity decays deterministically (inference from CAMPAIGN_STATE H-006 + PBO assumptions, https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=2326253).

## Hypotheses

### DR06-H1 — After pricing in the FULL Wave-1 search (all 90 logged configs, joint dependence, slip-1 costs), the Solar family contains at least one config with genuine superior predictive ability over a zero-P&L benchmark: Hansen SPA consistent p-value <= 0.05, and the Romano-Wolf StepM surviving set at FWE 0.10 is non-empty and includes at least one config inside the current SM 150-300 dense-scan region.

**Mechanism.** SPA bootstraps the max studentized statistic over the whole family so the search itself is under the null; studentization + recentering prevents the known-bad configs we honestly logged from diluting power (Hansen 2005). StepM then identifies WHICH configs survive with familywise error control, capturing the correlation of the 90 daily P&L streams (Romano-Wolf 2005). This is the correct formalization of 'is the family real or mined'.

**Experiment.** Pure Python, zero engine time. Assemble the T x 90 matrix of daily net P&L (slip-1 analytic overlay) from archived runs/*/daily_equity.parquet for every seq 1-90 config, aligned on exchange-session days over 2022-01..2026-07 (~1,150 days). Benchmark = 0 (primary) and the SW01b drift-matched random-entry median daily P&L (secondary, preregistered). Spec.yaml with benchmark definitions, B, block-length rule, and alpha committed BEFORE results are read, per campaign workflow.

Secondary read (drift-matched benchmark) is reported but only kills configs, never rescues them.

**Priority:** high · **Degrees of freedom:** 0 new strategy configs / 0 engine runs. Because the trial registry is frozen to ALL 90 logged configs, the known trial-padding exploit is structurally excluded.

**Experiment.** Pure Python. Output: PBO, logit distribution, IS-vs-OOS performance regression slope, stochastic dominance plot. Implementation cross-checked against the R 'pbo' package on a 10-config subsample to guard against coding error.

**Priority:** high · **Degrees of freedom:** 0 new configs. Analyst choices: S=16, performance functional (Sharpe), block alignment — 3 frozen choices. The trial set is not a choice: constitutionally all 90. · **Overfitting risk:** Low for the statistic itself; moderate interpretive risk — a low PBO under regime-mixing blocks (H-006 selectivity decay) can look better than forward reality, so PBO passes are necessary, never sufficient (flagged in not_transferable).

### DR06-H3 — The nominated Wave-1 config (whichever the plateau selector of DR06-H4 nominates, else the canonical 90/179) has DSR >= 0.95 under clustered effective trial count, and its Harvey-Liu haircut Sharpe remains > 0 under ALL of N in {N_eff_clustered, 90, 270}, computed from empirical skew/kurtosis of daily slip-1 P&L.

**Mechanism.** DSR = PSR evaluated at the expected-max null Sharpe implied by the trials actually run; PSR's Mertens variance correction means Solar's positive daily skew lowers the SR standard error, so this test is exact rather than conservatively Gaussian (Bailey-Lopez de Prado 2014). Bracketing N absorbs the unfixable uncertainty in effective trial count (correlated scans push N_eff down; unlogged design decisions push it up), per Harvey-Liu's demonstration that haircuts are nonlinear in N.

**Experiment.** Pure Python. (1) Correlation matrix of the 90 configs' daily P&L; average-linkage hierarchical clustering with preregistered cut at rho=0.7; N_eff = cluster count. (2) Compute annualized SR, skew, excess kurtosis of the nominated config's daily slip-1 P&L over full history; PSR(SR0=0); DSR with SR0 = E[max SR | N_eff, var of trial SRs]; Harvey-Liu haircut via their published code logic (Bonferroni, Holm, BHY) at N in {N_eff, 90, 270}. All formulas and the rho cut fixed in spec before the correlation matrix is computed.

**Pass/fail (preregistered).** PASS: DSR >= 0.95 at N_eff AND haircut SR > 0 under all three N brackets and all three adjustment methods at 5% significance. PARTIAL: DSR >= 0.95 at N_eff but haircut SR <= 0 at N=270 under BHY only — config promotable to Wave-2 with 'fragile significance' tag. FAIL: DSR < 0.90 at N_eff — config is not promotable regardless of net profit; leaderboard annotated accordingly.

**Priority:** medium · **Degrees of freedom:** 0 new configs. Analyst choices: clustering linkage + rho cut (2), N brackets (fixed by prior campaign protocol), significance level (1). The rho=0.7 cut is the weakest link — report N_eff sensitivity at rho in {0.5, 0.7, 0.9} as mandatory appendix, decisions keyed to 0.7 only. · **Overfitting risk:** Low-moderate: the clustering step is the one lore-adjacent component (ONC weakly validated); mitigated by bracketing and by making the raw-N=90 result binding in the GREY branch of DR06-H1.

### DR06-H4 — Under single-point parameter chaos, neighborhood-smoothed selection dominates argmax selection: on the archived dense SM scans (150..300 step 10, 16 configs each at 1m and 3m), a selector that maximizes the trimmed mean (drop min and max) of IS net P&L over the ±2-step neighborhood picks configs whose OOS rank beats the argmax selector's pick in >= 60% of CSCV splits, and the smoothed selector's PBO is lower by >= 0.10 in absolute terms on both timeframes.

**Mechanism.** Argmax on a discontinuous chaotic objective is a Breiman-unstable procedure; averaging over perturbations (here: parameter-neighborhood trimmed means, the spatial analogue of bagging) stabilizes selection and reduces predictive loss (Breiman 1996). The DC-recursion literature explains WHY pointwise net is chaotic — threshold changes re-partition the whole event sequence — so the neighborhood mean, not the point value, is the object with statistical meaning (Glattfelder et al. 2011; RE01 recovered math). This experiment measures the selection-stability gain directly instead of assuming it.

**Experiment.** Pure Python, zero engine time. For each timeframe's 16-config dense scan matrix (T days x 16): run the DR06-H2 CSCV machinery (S=16 blocks, all 12,870 splits); in each split apply selector A = argmax IS net, selector B = argmax over interior configs of trimmed-mean IS net across {theta-2..theta+2}; record both picks' OOS ranks and compute PBO_A, PBO_B. Neighborhood width (±2 steps = ±20 SM), trim rule (drop 1 high 1 low), and the two pass thresholds are fixed in this packet, before any split is computed. Edge configs (SM 150-160, 290-300) are excluded from B's argmax domain, preregistered.

**Pass/fail (preregistered).** PASS: B beats A on OOS rank in >= 60% of splits AND PBO_B <= PBO_A - 0.10, on BOTH 1m and 3m scans. If PASS: plateau/trimmed-neighborhood scoring becomes the campaign's mandatory selection functional for all future parameter scans, and single-config argmax results are barred from promotion gates. FAIL (either condition fails on either timeframe): retain argmax but require every promoted config to pass DR06-H1 StepM membership; the ±$40-90k chaos is then handled purely by multiple-testing gates rather than smoothing. INCONCLUSIVE is not permitted — thresholds are binary by construction.

**Priority:** high · **Degrees of freedom:** 0 new configs / 0 engine runs. Analyst choices: neighborhood width, trim rule, 60% and 0.10 thresholds — 4 choices, all frozen in this packet before execution. · **Overfitting risk:** Low: it is a methods experiment on already-burned data whose output is a selection RULE, not a config; the rule's parameters (width, trim) are fixed a priori and the dual-timeframe requirement acts as an internal replication.

### DR06-H5 — IID trade/day shuffling materially understates tail risk for this strategy: the 95th-percentile maximum drawdown of the canonical config's full-history slip-1 daily P&L under stationary bootstrap (corrected Politis-White block length, B=10,000) exceeds the 95th-percentile maxDD under IID daily shuffling by >= 20%.

**Mechanism.** Trend-following daily P&L exhibits serial dependence (losses cluster in chop regimes; SW01 showed high-vol tercile carries 58% of net), and block resampling preserves exactly the clustering that IID permutation destroys, so IID understates drawdown tails (Politis-Romano 1994 stationary bootstrap; Patton-Politis-White 2009 block length; practitioner demonstrations). Quantifying the gap calibrates which Monte Carlo is admissible for future risk gates and catastrophe-stop sizing (SW02).

**Experiment.** Pure Python on the canonical config's archived daily_equity.parquet: (a) IID permutation of daily P&L, B=10,000, record maxDD distribution; (b) stationary bootstrap with automatic block length on the same vector, B=10,000, record maxDD distribution; compare 95th percentiles. Also report the observed historical maxDD's percentile under each null as a diagnostic. Runtime minutes; spec committed first.

**Pass/fail (preregistered).** PASS (hypothesis confirmed): stationary-bootstrap 95th-pct maxDD >= 1.20 x IID 95th-pct maxDD. Consequence: IID shuffling is banned from campaign risk estimation; SW02 catastrophe-stop and expected-shortfall gates must use stationary bootstrap. FAIL: ratio < 1.20 — daily-level dependence is weak enough that either method is acceptable and the cheaper IID variant is permitted with a documented ratio. Either outcome updates infrastructure; neither promotes or kills a config.

**Priority:** medium · **Degrees of freedom:** 0 new configs. Analyst choices: B and the 1.20 threshold, both frozen here; block length is data-driven by the corrected automatic rule, not chosen. · **Overfitting risk:** Negligible — infrastructure calibration on one preexisting P&L vector with a preregistered binary threshold.

## Decision

Recommend admitting DR06-H1 (SPA + Romano-Wolf on the full 90-config matrix), DR06-H2 (CSCV/PBO of the selection procedure), and DR06-H4 (plateau vs argmax selection stability) to the active frontier immediately, in that logical order but runnable in one batch: all three are pure-Python analyses of already-archived daily P&L matrices, burn zero engine time and zero new configs, and together they convert the campaign's biggest open liability — 90 burned trials with NO remaining clean holdout after the Discovery Wave 1 directive removed the 2025-03→2026-07 reservation — into quantified, preregistered risk. H1 is the family-level existence test with the strongest peer-reviewed pedigree (Econometrica/JBES machinery, replicated for decades) and its hard-fail branch is the cheapest possible kill switch for the whole family. H2 tests the thing the ±$40-90k single-point chaos actually threatens — the selection procedure, not any single config. H4 is the packet's direct answer to 'what selection procedure is robust to threshold-recursion chaos': Breiman's instability theory predicts neighborhood-smoothed selection wins, and the experiment measures it with an internal 1m/3m replication; if it passes, trimmed-neighborhood scoring becomes the mandatory selection functional for every future scan. DR06-H3 (DSR + bracketed Harvey-Liu haircuts) should enter not as a standalone experiment but as the implementation of the already-adopted promotion gate — run it on whatever config H4's selector nominates; its clustering step is the only lore-adjacent component, so it must stay bracketed and subordinate to H1/H2. DR06-H5 is infrastructure calibration for SW02's risk gates; schedule it with SW02 rather than ahead of it. One standing caution binds all five: with the holdout gone and H-006's deterministic selectivity decay, every statistical pass here is a statement about selection integrity on 2022-2026 data, not a forecast — the campaign's constitution (all trials logged, gates preregistered before reads, trial registry frozen at all 90) is now the only thing that keeps these corrections honest, because PBO and DSR are both gameable by trial-set curation.
