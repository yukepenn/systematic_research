## What this wave established

1. **NQ genuinely exhibits the finance-literature leverage effect** (LEV01 Test 1) — a real,
   robust, externally-corroborated market-structure fact, confirmed across 5 canonical years.
   This is new, durable knowledge about the instrument itself, independent of any trading system.
2. **That fact does not translate into a usable trading signal for this system** — twice
   confirmed. LEV01 Test 2's apparently strong finding (t=4.45, cross-product-corroborated)
   collapsed to exactly zero once a sunk-P&L confound was identified and removed. LEV02's
   independently-designed, strictly causal version confirmed the null directly (no confound to
   find — there was simply nothing there). This is a valuable methodological result: it
   demonstrates the too-good-to-be-true gate catching a genuinely convincing false positive not
   once but with two independent confirmations.
3. **Return skewness (SKEW01) recorded the single worst right-tail failure of this entire
   campaign** — a naive filter on its strongest cell would have excluded 18 of the top-20
   all-time winning blocks, including the single largest trade in the dataset ($41,337.82).
4. **Combining Product A and Product B offers zero drawdown diversification benefit** (PORT01),
   precisely quantified for the first time: combined maxDD equals the exact naive sum of
   standalone maxDDs at every capital normalization tested. This reframes the practical path to
   capital efficiency: it has to come from new, lower-correlation alpha, not from portfolio
   construction on the current two objects.
5. **Product A's exposure-to-value mapping shows no evidence of mis-calibration** (EXP01) — a
   reassuring null that directly validates directive sec28's preference for the incumbent's
   simple linear mapping over a more complex alternative.
6. **A second literature scout pass corroborated, rather than extended, already-tested work** —
   the classic intraday U-shape liquidity pattern is real but already implicitly captured by this
   system's own session-phase state and U1's own findings.
