File: D:/OneDrive - Washington University in St. Louis/TradingResearch/systematic_research/research/original_trader_reconstruction/vendor_docs/volumedelta-manual.pdf

**signals_exposed**: The manual documents exactly ONE plotted value and gives it no programmatic name: "This indicator plots the volume delta of each bar." Its meaning is defined exactly: "The volume delta of a bar is equal to the total buy volume of the bar minus the total sell volume of the bar." NO Signal_* series, no public property names, no plot names, no colors, and no secondary values (cumulative delta, delta bars, min/max delta, etc.) are documented anywhere in the manual. The buy/sell volume components are defined per mode (see parameters) but are not documented as separately exposed series.

**entry_semantics**: NONE. The manual documents no entry rules of its own — no long/short conditions, no thresholds. The only trading guidance is Section 3 "Application", quoted in full: "One of the best uses of Volume Delta indicator is to confirm or filter a trading signal. It can be a great fit for any trading systems. For example, if your entry bar tells 'go long' but the volume delta of the bar is weak, then you should consider refusing the signal. This is just a very basic idea that needs further developments or enhancements. You will have to do your own research to build an effective strategy." Note: "weak" is never defined (no numeric threshold, no comparison rule, no lookback). The documented role is strictly confirmation/filter of an external signal, not signal generation.

**exit_semantics**: NONE. The manual contains zero exit, stop-loss, or profit-target guidance. The words stop/target/exit do not appear.

**parameters**: The manual documents exactly ONE parameter and states NO default for it: "Volume Base" (Section 2), an enum with 3 documented modes, in the manual's order: (1) BidAskPrice_RealVolume — "This mode should be used for instruments with real volumes (futures & stocks)." Buy: "If the price of a tick is greater than or equal to the ask price, the real volume of the tick is categorized as buy volume and added to the total buy volume of the bar that the tick belongs to." Sell: "If the price of a tick is less than or equal to the bid price, the real volume of the tick is categorized as sell volume and added to the total sell volume of the bar that the tick belongs to." Operational caveat quoted: "if Tick Replay is enabled, the indicator functions on both historical & real-time data... If Tick Replay is disabled, the indicator functions only on real-time data and there are no calculations on historical data. Any actions that lead to chart reloading will erase all calculations." (2) UpDownTick_RealVolume — for "instruments with real volumes (futures & stocks)". Buy: "If a tick is up or remains up, the real volume of the tick is categorized as buy volume..." Sell: "If a tick is down or remains down, the real volume of the tick is categorized as sell volume..." Caveat: "functions on both historical & real-time data. However... historical and real-time calculations of the same bar may not be 100% identical, due to technical limitations." (3) UpDownTick_UnitVolume — "the one & only choice for instruments without real volumes (forex, CFDs, indices)". Buy: "If a tick is up or remains up, a unit volume (1) is credited as buy volume for the tick..." Sell: "If a tick is down or remains down, a unit volume (1) is credited as sell volume for the tick..." Same historical/real-time caveat as mode 2. No other parameters (periods, smoothing, thresholds, colors, alerts) are documented, and no defaults are stated for anything — there is no parameter table or settings screenshot in the PDF.

**computable_in_python**: Fully specified and exactly implementable in Python GIVEN tick-level data: the core definition (bar delta = total buy volume − total sell volume) and all three per-tick classification rules are stated as exact inequalities/conditions. Mode 1 (BidAskPrice_RealVolume) needs ticks with concurrent bid/ask quotes: price >= ask → buy volume += tick volume; price <= bid → sell volume += tick volume. Modes 2/3 (UpDownTick) need only tick prices: uptick or zero-tick-following-uptick → buy; downtick or zero-tick-following-downtick → sell (standard tick rule / "tick test"); mode 3 replaces tick volume with 1. Under-specified edge cases (manual is silent; do not assume): (a) mode 1 gives no rule for ticks strictly inside the spread — as written they match neither inequality, i.e. are dropped from both sides — and no rule for locked/crossed markets where price could satisfy both; (b) which bid/ask snapshot pairs with the tick (before vs. after the trade) is unstated; (c) initial state of the up/down tick state machine (first tick of a session/series) is unstated; (d) tie-handling at exact bid==price==ask is undefined. Nothing in the math is proprietary/black-box — there are no secret formulas, periods, or smoothing; the only genuinely unverifiable parts are the vendor's NT8 implementation details behind those edge cases and the admitted historical-vs-realtime divergence ("due to technical limitations"). Practically, exact replication on 1-minute OHLCV bars is IMPOSSIBLE — every mode requires tick (and for mode 1, quote) data; on bar data only proxies exist, which would be our own construction, not the documented indicator.

**notes**: Manual is extremely thin: 2 pages, sections are 1. Overview, 2. Volume Base (2.1/2.2/2.3), 3. Application. Verified complete via text extraction AND full-page renders; only embedded image is the ninZa.co logo; only hyperlink is NT8 Tick Replay help (https://ninjatrader.com/support/helpGuides/nt8/en-us/?tick_replay.htm). For strategy-sleeve design: the vendor explicitly disclaims a tradable system ("You will have to do your own research to build an effective strategy"), so any delta-based entry/exit logic we build is independent by construction; the only vendor-anchored concepts are the three classification schemes and the bar-delta definition. The manual gives no basis for reconstructing Signal_* series names — if the deployed indicator exposes any, that knowledge must come from the assembly/UI, not this document. ## cumdelta

**product**: ninZa.co "Cumulative Delta" indicator for NinjaTrader 8 — "Cumulative Delta Trader Manual" (PDF, 2 pages, produced Microsoft Word 2019, creation date 2024-08-09, author metadata "PQH"). File: D:\OneDrive - Washington University in St. Louis\TradingResearch\systematic_research\research\original_trader_reconstruction\vendor_docs\cumulativedelta-manual.pdf

**signals_exposed**: THREE PLOTS (section 1): (1) "Cumulative Delta" — exact quote: '"Cumulative Delta": Provide raw data of cumulative delta which is identical to NinjaTrader 8's Order Flow+ Cumulative Delta indicator.' (2) "Moving Median" — exact quote: '"Moving Median": The moving median of the cumulative delta.' (3) "Difference" — exact quote: '"Difference": The difference between cumulative delta and moving median.' TWO NINJASCRIPT SIGNAL SERIES (section 3, "You can rely on the signals below to build your Bloodhound/Strategy."): (a) Signal_Trend — exact quote: 'Signal_Trend: 2 = uptrend strong, 1 = uptrend weak, -2 = downtrend strong, -1 = downtrend weak, 0 = neutral'. (b) Signal_State — exact quote: 'Signal_State: 1 = diff above positive threshold, -1 = diff below negative threshold, 0 = diff in between the thresholds'. No other public values are documented.

**entry_semantics**: The manual gives ONE example condition, explicitly labeled as an example and phrased permissively ("you can enter"), based on Signal_Trend transitions into the strong states. Exact quotes: 'Below is the example condition based on the Signal_Trend:' — LONG: 'If Signal_Trend switches from another value to 2 (Signal_Trend at the current bar equal to 2 and Signal_Trend at 1 bar ago not equal to 2), you can enter long here.' — SHORT: 'If Signal_ Trend switches from another value to -2 (Signal_ Trend at the current bar equal to -2 and Signal_ Trend at 1 bar ago not equal to -2), you can enter short here.' (The stray space in "Signal_ Trend" is in the original.) So the documented entry trigger is a first-bar transition INTO the ±2 strong-trend state, evaluated bar-over-bar. No entry rules based on Signal_State, the weak states (±1), or the raw plots are documented. The manual also points to a Strategy Builder tutorial video: https://youtu.be/mtMNjOQtfQE.

**exit_semantics**: NONE. The manual contains zero exit, stop-loss, profit-target, trailing, flatten-time, or position-management guidance of any kind. The only trade-management-adjacent text is qualitative sensitivity guidance on parameters: 'If the values of the 2 thresholds above are small, the market will easily enter a trend (with more whipsaws) and vice versa.' and (for the lookback) 'If its value is large, it will be difficult for the market to enter a trend, and vice versa if it is small, the market will easily enter a trend, and there are whipsaw signals on your chart.' Any exit design for our sleeves must therefore be our own choice, not a vendor-documented rule.

**parameters**: In the manual's exact order (section 2). IMPORTANT: NO default values are stated anywhere in the manual — there are no parameter screenshots (the only embedded image is the ninZa.co logo), so defaults are unknowable from this document. (2.1) "Volume Base" — exact quote: 'Can choose from BidAskPrice_RealVolume or UpDownTick_RealVolume or UpDownTick_UnitVolume.' Details of these modes are delegated to an external link: https://drive.google.com/file/d/1bRPF2oOBNLA4ru3ds4Ke0oQIfikmImLH/view?pli=1 (not contained in the PDF). (2.2) "Threshold: Positive Strong" — 'The positive strong threshold of the difference.' and "Threshold: Negative Strong" — 'The negative strong threshold of the difference.' with the whipsaw-sensitivity note quoted in exit_semantics. (2.3) "Moving Median: Period" — 'The period of the Moving Median.' (2.4) "Cumulative Delta Lookback (Bars)" — 'The number of bars to monitor cumulative delta for trend shifts.' with the large-vs-small sensitivity note quoted in exit_semantics. Total: 5 user parameters (Volume Base, Threshold Positive Strong, Threshold Negative Strong, Moving Median Period, Cumulative Delta Lookback Bars); every default is undocumented.

**computable_in_python**: FULLY SPECIFIED (implementable exactly in Python given tick/bid-ask data): (1) Cumulative Delta plot — declared 'identical to NinjaTrader 8's Order Flow+ Cumulative Delta indicator', i.e., running sum of per-bar delta; with Volume Base = BidAskPrice_RealVolume this is the standard at-ask-minus-at-bid classified real volume, and the UpDownTick variants are standard uptick/downtick classification with real vs unit (per-tick count) volume — all standard order-flow math, though the manual itself defers mode definitions to the external Google Drive link, and session-reset behavior is inherited from NT8's Order Flow+ convention rather than restated. (2) Moving Median plot — rolling median of the cumulative-delta series over "Moving Median: Period" bars; exact standard math. (3) Difference plot — CumulativeDelta minus MovingMedian; exact. (4) Signal_State — pure threshold classification of Difference against Threshold: Positive Strong / Threshold: Negative Strong (only unstated detail: strict vs non-strict inequality exactly at a threshold). PROPRIETARY / BLACK-BOX: Signal_Trend — the manual never specifies the rule mapping (Difference, thresholds, Cumulative Delta Lookback (Bars)) to the five states {2,1,0,-1,-2}; "uptrend strong" vs "uptrend weak" distinction, what constitutes a "trend shift" over the lookback window, and how/when the state resets to 0 are all undocumented; only directional sensitivity guidance (small thresholds / small lookback → trend flips more easily, more whipsaws) is given. Any Python reconstruction of Signal_Trend is necessarily our own hypothesis, not the vendor's documented rule.
