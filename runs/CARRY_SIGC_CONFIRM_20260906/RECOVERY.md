## 1. Exact signal construction (`runs/CARRY_V1_20260828/SPEC.md`, `src/carry_v1.py`)

- **Universe** (frozen by CARRY00 on coverage alone, before any P&L): 10 roots, 4 sectors — equity_index {ES,YM}, rates {ZN,ZB}, metals {GC,SI}, ags {ZC,ZW,ZM,ZL} (`carry_v1.py:45-46`). NG was carry-capable but sectorless (§40 needs ≥2 roots); FX/NQ/ZT/ZF/CL/RTY/RB/HO/HG were data-blocked (`runs/CARRY00_CURVE_DATA_CAPABILITY_20260828/REPORT.md` §2).
- **Signal**: `curve_slope = (P_near − P_deferred) / month_gap`; `carry_score = curve_slope / σ`, σ = **strictly lagged** 63-day sd of the basis-safe daily price change (`shift(1)`, `carry_v1.py:129-133`). Difference form chosen (pre-committed) to survive negative prices; `month_gap` normalization because gaps are not uniform across sectors (equity/rates 3mo, metals 2mo, ZM/ZL 1mo — `out/carry_v1.txt:4-13`).
- **Near/deferred**: near = the causally-designated active contract from the volume-crossover roll ledger; deferred = the nearest later listed month **with a bar on that date** among cached contracts (`carry_v1.py:110-125`).
- **Weights**: carry_score centred-ranked **within sector** to [−1,+1]: `w_raw = 2(rank−1)/(n−1) − 1`. With n=2 this degenerates to a **binary ±1 switch** — true for 3 of 4 sectors including metals (REPORT §5). Then `w = w_rank / (n_live_sectors × n_sector)` (equal risk across live sectors), units = `w/σ` (risk-normalized, lagged σ) (`carry_v1.py:179,199`).
- **Rebalance**: WEEKLY; ISO week weights use the last observation strictly before the week's first trading day; a pair observation staler than 7 days ⇒ root dropped; sector with <2 observed roots ⇒ CASH (`carry_v1.py:138-176`).
- **Roll / P&L substrate**: causal volume-crossover roll (prior-day volumes only, one-way, 5-day pre-expiry buffer) + the s7 self-financing economic return (overnight on old contract, intraday on target; never differences two contracts) — `research/multi_market/src/roll.py`. Unit tests: basis invariance, no-roll telescoping, roll causality — all passed at 0.000e+00 (REPORT §3).
- **Costs**: $4.36 RT commission + 1 tick (PRIMARY) / 2 ticks (STRESS), charged only to position changes (`carry_v1.py:49-50,203-205`). TICKSZ GC 0.10 ($10/tick @ PV 100), SI 0.005 ($25/tick @ PV 5,000) (`carry_v1.py:56-57`, `ncd_day.py:81-86`).
- **Causality**: two-sided perturbation probe (corrupt future ⇒ weights must not move: 0.000e+00; corrupt past ⇒ must move: 2.5e−01) — both PASS (`out/carry_v1.txt:15-18`).
