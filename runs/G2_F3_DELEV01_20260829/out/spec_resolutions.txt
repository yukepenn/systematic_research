R1. SESSION UNIVERSE (U) AND RTH ANCHORS
    Bars are END-stamped; session label per session_boundary convention (bar stamped <=17:00 ->
    same-calendar-day label; >17:00 -> next day). RTH close of session s = close of the bar stamped
    exactly 16:00:00 on date s (covers 15:59-16:00). RTH open of session s = OPEN of the bar stamped
    exactly 09:31:00 (= the 09:30:00 price). U = the set of sessions possessing a 16:00-stamped bar.
    Half/short sessions (no 16:00 bar, e.g. 13:00 closes) are NOT in U: they host no events, do not
    count in the 3-session horizon, and are never entry/exit sessions. Frozen simplification, stated
    not searched. Uniqueness of the 09:31/16:00 bar per session is asserted.

R2. WINDOW AND WARM-UP
    Substrate: research/scalping_lab/substrate/minute/NQ/nq1m_2005_202605.parquet ONLY (single
    continuous source; sha256 verified against runs/GENESIS_REPRO_INCUMBENT_20260828/out/
    run_provenance.txt and printed). Events restricted to U-sessions labeled in
    [2006-01-01, 2026-05-31]. 2005 data is warm-up only (prev close, MA200, RV21). The substrate
    ends before the burn window (2026-05-31 -> 2026-07-31); max session label is printed as proof.
    An event must be POLICY-COMPLETABLE: next U-session exists with a 09:31 bar, and the t+3 U-close
    is inside the window. Otherwise it is UNTRADEABLE — dropped with a printed count.

R3. RETURN / MA / BAND / BEAR (all frozen, computed once)
    r_t = close_t / close_{t-1} - 1 over CONSECUTIVE U-sessions. Event band: -0.05 <= r_t < -0.025.
    MA200_t = simple mean of the 200 U-closes ending at and INCLUDING t (min_periods=200).
    Bear filter: close_t < MA200_t, strict.

R4. POLICY
    SHORT 1 contract at open of the 09:31 bar of the next U-session (t+1); exit at close of the
    16:00 bar of the 3rd U-session AFTER the event (t+3) — "3 sessions later" counts from the event
    session; the hold spans U-sessions t+1..t+3. PV = $20/pt. net_usd = (entry - exit) * 20 - 33.
    $33/RT is the whole friction (spec: stressed tape), applied identically to events, controls and
    pseudo-events.

R5. GAP-DOWN VETO (S1-10, frozen)
    Veto iff open_{t+1}/close_t - 1 < -0.015 (strictly MORE than 1.5% below; exactly -1.5% is not
    vetoed). D1 population = TRADED events (completable and not vetoed). Printed counts:
    detected / untradeable / vetoed / traded.

R6. D1 — "EVENT-CLUSTERED t"
    Resolved conservatively (fewer, larger clusters -> wider SE -> harder to pass): clusters =
    connected components of the graph joining two traded events if (a) their hold windows
    [t+1, t+3] share any session (positions <= 2 apart in U), OR (b) their event labels fall in the
    same calendar month. t = mean/SE from OLS-on-constant with CR1 cluster-robust covariance
    (statsmodels), compared to 2.0 as specced. PASS iff mean net > 0 AND t >= 2.0.
    MDE = 2.0 * clustered SE (the smallest mean detectable at the t-gate), printed together with
    per-era event counts BEFORE any return table.

R7. ERAS (reporting only, no gate): 2006-07, 2008-09, 2010-13, 2014-17, 2018-19, 2020-21, 2022,
    2023-2026/05. The concentration of effective N in 2008/2020/2022 is stated in the output.

R8. D2 — VOL-MATCHED CONTROL
    RV_t = ddof-1 std of the 21 U-session returns ending at and INCLUDING t (includes the event
    day's own return — the stricter control: control days sit in regimes reflecting the event's own
    vol contribution; harder for D2 to pass, hence conservative). Decile edges = np.quantile deciles
    of RV over ALL in-window U-sessions with valid RV. Control pool = in-window U-sessions with
    r < 0, valid RV, NOT a detected event day, policy-completable AND veto-passing (the same policy
    including the veto — gives an exact count match on TRADED events per decile). Per-decile
    sampling without replacement, ascending decile order, from the SINGLE shared
    numpy.random.default_rng(0) Generator — consumed BEFORE D3's draw. Pool shortfall in a decile:
    take all, print the shortfall. D2 PASS iff event mean net > control mean net (strict). No
    auxiliary re-draws are computed (single specced draw; nothing that could blur the gate).

R9. D3 — CIRCULAR-SHIFT NULL
    Universe = the in-window U-session position array (size N). The DETECTED-event indicator (band
    AND bear, pre-veto — the same object the real pipeline starts from) is circularly shifted by k;
    at shifted positions the full policy is applied (completability + veto), statistic = mean net
    over traded pseudo-events. 500 distinct shifts (>= 300 specced) drawn from {1..N-1} without
    replacement from the SHARED rng AFTER the D2 draw. Positions whose policy window would cross
    the array edge are simply not completable (dropped; no price wrap). Shifts with zero traded
    pseudo-events are excluded with a printed count. PASS iff observed D1 mean net > 95th
    percentile (numpy linear interpolation) of the shift means.
