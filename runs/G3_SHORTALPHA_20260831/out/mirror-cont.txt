    Required N for power 1-beta:   N = 213 * ( SE(213) * (z_0.95 + z_{1-beta}) / DELTA_true )^2

    ---- WHAT THIS MEANS OPERATIONALLY -------------------------------------------------
    1. NO DEPLOYMENT IS NEEDED. MIRROR's forward weekly $ is a deterministic function of
       NQ 1-min bars. Freezing run_we_w118.scan/econ + the four literals and hash-
       registering them BEFORE the read gives a legitimate PRE-FROZEN forward series. A
       .cs, an account, and fills add execution realism that GATE 2 DOES NOT TEST.
    2. THE PAPER STREAM CAN SUPPLY THE BOOK SIGN, and that is a real (small) contribution:
       99.5 % sign agreement with the research book. But it supplies HALF a statistic. The
       MIRROR leg has to be simulated regardless, and once you are simulating one leg on
       sealed bars you may as well simulate both - which is cheaper, is not roll-gapped,
       and does not have to wait for the paper account. The paper stream is therefore
       NEITHER NECESSARY NOR SUFFICIENT here. It is not the bottleneck.
    3. THE PAPER STREAM IS ALSO CONTAMINATED FOR THIS PURPOSE IN ITS FIRST MONTH: the
       contract roll blocks NEW ENTRIES for XM from 2026-09-06 and P1 from 2026-09-08,
       with safe re-enable P1 >= 09-17 / XM >= 09-19. That is a ~2-week hole in the book
       leg that has no counterpart in the MIRROR leg. Weeks 2026-W37..W38 are unusable.
    4. THE SHADOW LEDGER CANNOT HOLD IT ANYWAY. shadow_ledger.py refuses rows at or before
       SHADOW_START and refuses non-advancing timestamps; a simulated MIRROR series is a
       backtest overlay, not a decision-first row. It belongs under LOCKED_FORWARD.md as a
       preregistered one-shot read, NOT in the prospective shadow.

    ==> ANSWER TO (c): NO DEPLOYMENT IS NEEDED and none should be built. The correct
        forward instrument is a preregistered, hash-frozen SIMULATED read of both legs on
        sealed bars - not a .cs, not the paper account, not the shadow ledger. The paper
        stream can corroborate the book leg (99.5 % sign agreement) but is not required
        and is roll-gapped in its first month. The ONLY binding constraint is CALENDAR
        TIME - and section 6 shows the required calendar time does not exist.

    THE DECISION-READY ANSWER
======================================================================================================================

    (a) The object is fully specified with zero remaining choices (section a). The one
        non-obvious requirement is 250 sessions of causal warm-up, which a cold forward
        start does NOT supply for its first ~190 decisions.

    (b) The forward statistic is DELTA = mean(MIRROR wk | book wk < 0) - mean(MIRROR wk).
        Observed DELTA = $-49 +/- $353. IT IS NEGATIVE.
        REQUIRED FORWARD N AT THE OBSERVED EFFECT SIZE: INFINITE. The gate cannot be
        passed by waiting, because the estimate is on the wrong side of zero and more
        data tightens it AROUND A NEGATIVE NUMBER.
        Even at the 95 % CI UPPER LIMIT ($+644/wk - the most generous effect the
        data still permits) the answer is 397 forward weeks = 7.6 YEARS at 80 % power.
        The honest sentence is not 'three years'. It is 'longer than the evidence would
        stay relevant, for an effect the data already estimates as negative'.
