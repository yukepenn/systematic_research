=== PHASE 4c - THE ORACLE BOUND. How big is the prize an exit rule is chasing?
======================================================================================================================
   Perfect foresight: close every trade at its own MFE bar. Not attainable - it is the
   CEILING on any exit repair whatsoever, and it names the level (per OPPORTUNITY_LANGUAGE:
   this is a PERFECT-FORESIGHT-EXIT ceiling on the mirrored short sleeve, 2022-07..2026-05,
   1 contract, PRIMARY cost - it is not an achievable figure and may not be quoted as one).
   It is INFLATED twice over: MFE is read off bar EXTREMES, which no market order can be
   filled at, and it assumes foresight of which bar that is. It is used here only as a
   DENOMINATOR, so both inflations work AGAINST the closure - they make the recovered
   share look smaller. The closure survives them by three orders of magnitude.
