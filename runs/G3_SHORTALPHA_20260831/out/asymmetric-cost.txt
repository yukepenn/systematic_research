WHAT THIS RUN SHOWS, AND WHAT IT DOES NOT
============================================================================================================
SHOWS:
  1. The MECHANISM is real and survives its controls. Shorts do fire and exit in higher-vol
     states than longs (vol@exit 0.000328 vs 0.000296; 64.8% vs 58.5% of exits are into
     rising vol), the differential is
     concentrated on the EXIT leg (G4), it is largest for the short's WINNERS (G5, +0.412 tk),
     it is NOT explained by hour-of-day (N2: only +0.07 of +1.15 $/ctrRT is hour), and it
     sits far outside the state-blind label-shuffle null (G6).
  2. The DOLLARS are small. +1.15 $/ctrRT, 95% CI [+0.58, +1.70]. That is 2.1% of the
     short sleeve's $54.59/trade commission-only gross. The preregistered materiality bar was
     $5.00/ctrRT and it is MISSED by a factor of 4.4.
  3. The DIRECT measurement does not confirm even that. On 429 real fill instants it is
     -0.63 $/ctrRT, CI [-3.22, +2.06] -- the WRONG SIGN, and consistent with zero. G3 FAILS.
  4. Every non-execution asymmetry an equity short would face prices at EXACTLY $0 in an
     index FUTURE: no borrow, no locate, no dividend liability, no SSR, side-symmetric
     margin, and both arms flat at the close so no overnight financing either.
DOES NOT SHOW:
  a. It does not measure a pre-2022 spread. No NQ quote below NQ 23,036 exists in this repo.
  b. The E2 LEVEL for 2022-2024 is not quotable (tick-denominated spread across a 2x price
     range). Only the SHORT-minus-LONG differential is defended, and that differential is itself
     unstable year to year (-0.01 to +0.58 tk).
  c. It does not test whether a short-specific ENTRY or EXIT POLICY could avoid the wide-spread
     states. That would be an exposure-reducing rule and would require a state-blind random-
     thinning control, which this repo's record says it would lose (11 for 11). None is proposed.
  d. It does not revise the repo's $20.65/ctrRT. It says that number applies to the short side
     too, within about a dollar.

THE ONE NUMBER THAT DID MOVE, AND IT IS NOT A COST-ASYMMETRY NUMBER:
   At the SAME measured $20.65/ctrRT, the short sleeve pays 37.8% of its own gross in spread
   against the long object's 15.8% -- 2.40x -- purely because it needs 2225 contract round

   quote this sleeve COMMISSION-ONLY. Its honest post-spread standalone figure is
