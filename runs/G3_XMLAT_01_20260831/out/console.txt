  METHOD. The sub-second slippage measured in X3-A on n = 33 sessions is
  applied to ALL 371 trades at 1.55 trades/week. That is an EXTRAPOLATION
  from a 2025-08-11..2026-07-31 tick sample to a

  population. The BANDS below are therefore the UNION of (i) the block/percentile
