    6E            71                                      67

    THE CAUSE.  V5 is `median(last 5 bars) / median(the 63 bars before them)`.  For a
    QUARTERLY financial (ES 134 bars, ZN, GC ...) the 63-bar baseline sits INSIDE the
    contract's front-month life, so the ratio correctly measures collapse into expiry.
    For MONTHLY energy, NT8 cached a MEDIAN OF 26 DAILY BARS PER CL CONTRACT, so for the
    few CL/NG contracts long enough to be measured at all, the 63-bar baseline falls in the
    contract's DEFERRED, quiet period and the last 5 days fall near FRONT-MONTH status.
    The ratio is then measuring the RISE INTO front month, not a failure to collapse.

    worked example  CL 11-12  (126 bars, 2012-04-30 -> 2012-10-22, expiry 2012-10-22)
        final 5 daily volumes      [102195, 103382, 96179, 34805, 5456]
        -> the LAST TWO DAYS DO collapse (34,805 then 5,456); the 5-day MEDIAN (96,179)
           is dominated by the three front-month days that precede them
        63-bar baseline before     6,650  -- the contract's DEFERRED period
