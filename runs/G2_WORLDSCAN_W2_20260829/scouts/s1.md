# S1 — SHORT-NATIVE MECHANISM SCOUT (GENESIS II WORLD SCAN WAVE 2)

DOMAIN SUMMARY (3 lines):
Short-side alpha on index futures is not mirrored-long: the robust academic finding is that DOWNSIDE moves carry the persistent volatility content (Patton-Sheppard) and that mechanical, price-insensitive flows (leveraged-ETF and option-gamma rebalancing, vol-ETP NAV rebalancing, margin-spiral deleveraging) trade WITH the move into the close — so the short-native edge concentrates in the LAST HOUR of big down days and in mid-magnitude (2.5–5%) bear-regime drops, while extreme gap-downs and vol-spike closes systematically BOUNCE at the daily horizon (fire-sale reversal is documented at the account level).
Best structural observables we already own: VX/VXM 1-min (never used), internals minute 2022+ (Steenbarger-style adjusted-TICK divergence: up-days with weak breadth were the short, −0.24% next 4d vs +0.39%), and NQ 1-min for close-to-15:00 → last-hour continuation regressions with era splits.
