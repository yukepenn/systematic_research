# S4-OVERNIGHT — GENESIS II WORLD SCAN WAVE 2 (representation-shifted)

DOMAIN SUMMARY (3 lines):
1. The strongest external evidence says the overnight edge is not "the night" but three narrow, state-conditioned windows: the European-open hour (inventory-reversal after prior-day sell-offs, Boyarchenko et al.), the 30 minutes before 08:30 releases (pre-announcement drift ≈ half the total adjustment, Kurov et al.), and event-conditioned Sunday/earnings reopens (weekend-crypto and after-hours-earnings information that only prices at 18:00).
2. Gap folklore inverts: the peer-reviewed 1928-2018 index result is gap-day MOMENTUM with "gaps get filled" explicitly unsupported — the opposite of retail doctrine, and testable against our ON-range-state conditioning in one wave.
3. Trader-land's live representation shift is partitioning the overnight into Asia/London sub-session extremes (ICT killzones) rather than one night channel — public NQ/MES code exists with (tiny-sample) results; prior is LOW given our sweep-and-reclaim death, but the observable is genuinely different from W96 NIGHT.
