WHAT THE HEADLINE PROBABILITY IS OVER  [G4, in words, before any number]
  P(ruin) = P( the cumulative realised P&L path reaches or passes below -EQUITY at ANY
            point within the horizon ), i.e. THE ACCOUNT IS WIPED OUT.

  start, so that number is strictly larger.  Calling it ruin is the 10x error CAP01
  published on 2026-09-01.  Both are printed side by side in every table below.
  It is NOT P(margin call), reported separately on the day-margin floor.
