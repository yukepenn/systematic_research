**REVIVAL CONDITION, specific and monitorable: the short sleeve's trailing-24-month t returns to
the median of its own history (≈ +2.1) — equivalently, 2026's −10.62 pts/session reverses.** At

doubles the median week for +7 % money, and only the single worst week gets worse.

`INFERENCE` worth carrying: a long-only trend engine and its mirror decaying in *opposite*
phases is what a directional regime looks like. 2026 has been a strong up-market — P1's best
recent years and the short sleeve's worst. If that is the mechanism, the short sleeve is
insurance whose premium is currently being paid, not a broken engine. **That is a hypothesis,
not a finding, and it has not been tested.**
