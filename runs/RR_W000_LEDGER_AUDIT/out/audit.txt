--- FINDING 2: E_NO_ENGINE = 0 is FORCED BY CONSTRUCTION, not measured ---
    W119 spec defines E_NO_ENGINE as 'neither leg held a position while the session's
    absolute RTH move was in its own top decile'.
    top-decile |RTH move| threshold        : 295.0 pts   (NaN moves: 46)
    sessions with NEITHER leg active       :   282
    ... AND in the top |move| decile       :    32   <- E_NO_ENGINE on the RAW mask
    ... AND ALSO book_pnl < 0              :     0   <- what the report printed
    distinct book_pnl values on the raw mask: [0.0]

    A session in which neither leg holds a position has book_pnl == 0 by definition and can
    NEVER satisfy book_pnl < 0. The lens was evaluated INSIDE the losing-session population,
    so it was empty before any data was read. It measured nothing.
    On the raw mask: n = 32, mean |RTH move| 452.2 pts, mean RTH range 587.9 pts

    figure attached to them is EX_POST_EXECUTION_FEASIBLE_ORACLE (level 2) and NOT available
