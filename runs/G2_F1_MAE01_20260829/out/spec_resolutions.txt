R2. TRADE LIFE FOR 1-MIN BAR EXTREMES (bars END-stamped; entry at open of entry bar)
    gfills fills: entry at o[ei] of the entry bar (et = t[ei]); flip exits at o[xi] of the exit
    bar (xt = t[xi]); session-end flatten exits at c[xi] of the lb bar. Exit type is recovered
    per trade by exact inversion of the stored pnl: px = epx + (pnl + COMM_RT*u)/(d*u*PV),
    matched against o[xi] / c[xi] (tolerance 1e-6). Life window for extremes:
      OPEN-exit trades:  full ranges of bars ei..xi-1, PLUS the single exit price o[xi].
      CLOSE-exit trades: full ranges of bars ei..xi (position held through the flatten bar).
    The entry bar's FULL range counts (entry is at its open; intrabar ordering unknowable at
    bar resolution — conservative for MAE). A trade that cannot be resolved (no timestamp
    match, or pnl inversion matches neither o nor c) is UNMEASURED and counts against M1.

R3. MAE / MFE DEFINITIONS
    Long:  MAE = max(0, epx - min(lows in life)),  MFE = max(0, max(highs in life) - epx).
    Short: MAE = max(0, max(highs in life) - epx), MFE = max(0, epx - min(lows in life)).
    Reported PER CONTRACT in points and in dollars (points * PV, PV = $20.0/pt). Position-level
    dollars (* u) are additionally reported for context; the spec's headline unit is
    per-contract. Excursions are GROSS of commission and spread (price path only).

R4. OUTCOME CLASS / SIZE CLASS
    Outcome: WIN if stored trade pnl > 0 else LOSS (pnl as frozen: gross move * PV * u minus
    commission COMM_RT*u; spread friction not in the stored pnl). Size: u == 1 vs u == 2.
    Distributions reported for ALL, WIN, LOSS, U1, U2 and the four crosses.

R5. TICK CALIBRATION (bar-vs-tick bias)
    Eligible sessions = sessions of in-window P1/PCT trades whose session date (YYYYMMDD) is in
    research/data_esnq/ALLOWLIST_DEV_44.txt AND research/data_esnq/parquet/NQ/s<date>.parquet
    exists. Allowlist membership is asserted and PRINTED for every file opened; seal_guard
    checks every time column. Trade events are the bip category whose per-session row count
    matches the session manifest's 'trades' column (identified programmatically, printed).
    Tick life window mirrors R2 in exchange time: entry instant = t[ei]-60s;
      OPEN-exit: ticks in [t[ei]-60s, t[xi]-60s) plus the first trade tick at/after t[xi]-60s;
      CLOSE-exit: ticks in [t[ei]-60s, t[xi]).
    tick epx = first trade tick at/after the entry instant. Calibration factor reported BOTH as
    ratio of means (mean tick_MAE / mean bar_MAE; same for MFE) and mean paired difference in
    points, with per-session bar-vs-tick entry-price offset as an adjustment diagnostic.
    THRESHOLD (fixed now, before overlap is computed): if overlapping measured trades N < 10,
    calibration is SKIPPED honestly and only N is reported (gate M2 = N reported either way).
    THE FACTOR IS APPLIED TO NOTHING — Stage 1 tables stay bar-based (spec: "apply nothing
    silently" -> nothing is applied at all; the factor is reported alongside).

R6. STAGE 2 STOP MECHANICS (300 pt disaster stop, per contract = price excursion of 300 pts)
    For each frozen trade independently, scan life bars in chronological order (OPEN-exit:
    ei..xi-1; CLOSE-exit: ei..xi). First bar j where the adverse extreme reaches the level
    (long: l[j] <= epx-300; short: h[j] >= epx+300) fires the stop BEFORE any favorable exit
    in that bar (conservative intrabar ordering, incl. beating the flatten on a CLOSE-exit
    bar). Fill = worse-of(level, bar open): long min(epx-300, o[j]); short max(epx+300, o[j]).
    New pnl = d*u*(fill-epx)*PV - COMM_RT*u; new exit time t[j]. "No other change": all other
    trades, entries, and the session-box sequence stay FROZEN (no re-entry, no box knock-on) —
    a stated overlay limitation, printed in the output. A gap-through open on the frozen
    OPEN-exit bar itself equals the frozen exit price and is left as the frozen exit.

R7. "TOTAL NET" / "DELTA NET" (decision clause 1)
    net(trade) = stored pnl - rate*u, where rate is the candidate-specific spread $/ctrRT from
    the recorded net_series formula (W82 per-minute profile weighted by this trade list's own
    fill minutes, u-weighted, entry+exit). Baseline rate from frozen fills; variant rate
    RECOMPUTED from post-stop fill minutes (both printed). total net = sum over the 2,131
    trades of net(trade), baseline. delta net = variant total - baseline total. Clause 1 PASS
    iff delta_net >= -0.01 * total_net_baseline (baseline asserted > 0).

R8. "TRADE-LEVEL maxDD" (decision clause 2)
    Max peak-to-trough drawdown of the cumulative NET per-trade equity curve (trades in
    chronological entry order; net as in R7, each side using its own rate). Improvement =
    (maxDD_base - maxDD_variant) / maxDD_base; clause 2 PASS iff >= 0.10.
