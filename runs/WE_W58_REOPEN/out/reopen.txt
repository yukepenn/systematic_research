======================================================================================================================
'It works in the last two years' is trivially satisfiable. The question that is not
trivial is whether the LATEST window is representative of the sleeve's own history.

Compared on the SCALE-FREE t, not on dollars: NQ's price and daily range grew ~6x over
this sample, so a dollar comparison across decades measures the instrument, not the edge.

   'eff. indep.' is windows / 24: consecutive 24-month windows stepped monthly
   overlap by 23/24, so the effective number of independent observations is that many.
   A sleeve whose LATEST t sits at the top of its own t-history is having an
   exceptional run, not demonstrating a durable edge - and 'recent effectiveness' is
   then being satisfied by luck. That is this wave's preregistered falsifier.
