R1. Window mapping (END-stamped bars, CLAUDE.md §6). Anchor 15:50: pre-window = bars
    stamped 15:31:00..15:40:00 inclusive (trading 15:30-15:40), post-window = bars

R2. Session grid. Per session_id (calendar date of the 16:00 close; RTH stamps all fall
    on the session_id date), closes are reindexed onto the full RTH grid of stamps
    09:30:00..16:00:00 (391 points; forward-fill from last raw close <= stamp, seeded by
    the last pre-09:30 close of the same session). Returns = grid close diffs (390
    values stamped 09:31..16:00, POINTS — substrate law). Volume = raw per-minute volume
    aligned to grid stamps; minutes with no trades = 0 volume, 0 return (a no-trade
    minute is genuinely zero activity, not missing data).

R3. Session inclusion. A session qualifies iff (a) >= 300 raw bars stamped within
    09:31-16:00, and (b) >= 1 raw bar stamped within 15:56-16:00 (excludes early-close
    days), and (c) >= 1 raw close at/before 09:30 for the return seed. Range: session_id
    2006-01-01 .. 2026-05-31. Load passes research_sdk.seal_guard.assert_presealed.

R4. Statistics (per anchor, per window; per-session value then unweighted cross-session
    mean over the full 2006..2026-05 sample — the frozen break test is full-sample; the
    era split belongs only to the conditional reversion table):
      S1 mean |1-min return| (points): session mean of |ret| over the 10 window bars.
      S2 mean volume: session mean of the 10 window bars' volume.
      S3 lag-1 return autocorr: within-session Pearson corr of (r_t, r_{t+1}) over the
         window's 10 returns (9 pairs); sessions with zero variance in either vector are

    EFFECT at anchor T, statistic S = | mean_post(S) - mean_pre(S) | (absolute
    difference of cross-session aggregates; magnitude, because the frozen clause is
    "the 15:50 effect exceeds ..." — a magnitude comparison).
