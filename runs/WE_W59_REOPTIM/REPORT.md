`FACT`: **the traded-day positive rate is monotone in the target — 49.3 → 47.3 → 46.0 → 44.0 →
42.2 → 41.7 — and pts/session moves the opposite way.** The hypothesis this wave was built on
is confirmed exactly: the target does not create money, it **converts** money into green days.

The halt is monotone too, and in the same direction:

day red; without it, some of those days recover. The halt earns its keep on
money-per-drawdown ($1,475 vs $759) and pays for it in every consistency metric.

## 4. SELECT versus AGGREGATE — the law holds, and it acquires a boundary

| arm | day + % | traded-day + % | week + % | wk streak | median week | weekly $ | mean top-5 DD |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **P1 incumbent cell** | 27.6 | 46.0 | 58.3 | 8 | $455 | **$1,475** | $14,266 |
| S2 aggregate of all 216 | 27.4 | 43.7 | 58.8 | 7 | $438 | $909 | **$10,722** |
| (the average single cell) | 26.4 | 45.1 | 56.2 | 7 | $331 | $879 | $12,219 |
| **S3 aggregate over HALT only** | **29.0** | **48.3** | **59.3** | 7 | $336 | $1,249 | **$12,297** |
| S3 aggregate over VOTE only | 27.1 | 43.2 | 57.4 | **6** | $461 | $1,471 | $14,371 |
| S3 aggregate over TARGET only | 27.2 | 45.3 | 58.3 | 8 | $367 | $1,443 | $14,631 |
| **S1 walk-forward selector** | 25.9 | 45.7 | 55.4 | 8 | $192 | **$462** | $9,008 |
| S4 hindsight best, NOT achievable | 30.5 | **50.9** | 59.8 | **5** | **$767** | $1,445 | $12,455 |

**`REPRODUCED` — selection is worse than noise.** The walk-forward selector, choosing each
quarter the cell that was best on trailing data by the consistency objective, earns **$462/week
against a random-choice null mean of $897 — the 2.0th percentile.** It never once chose the
incumbent and changed its mind in 5 of 12 live quarters. W19 measured PBO 0.48–0.90 for
selection among the 32 inner configs; this is the same result on the outer parameters and it is
worse.

**The preregistered prediction was right: the aggregate beats the selector ($909 vs $462).**

**And the law acquires a boundary it did not have.** The aggregate of all 216 also *loses to the
incumbent* ($909 vs $1,475), because it is pulled toward the average cell ($879):

> `INFERENCE`, stated as such: **aggregation helps when the members are near-exchangeable in
> quality, and dilutes when there is a real quality gradient.** The 32 inner configs are
> near-exchangeable — they are the same rule at different VolMults. The 216 outer cells are not:
> the money metric has a strong gradient across them. Aggregate the axis, not the object.
> **Aggregating over the HALT alone** — where the cells genuinely are near-exchangeable and
> different halts fire on different days — gives **+2.3 pp traded-day, +1.0 pp weekly, −14 % on
> the drawdown distribution, for −15 % money, with no parameter chosen at any point.**

## 5. The one direction the evidence actually supports

S4's hindsight winner is **h2000_t750** — a *one-step* move from the incumbent on both axes, in
the direction that phase 1 showed **monotonically** on 4 and 6 levels respectively. A monotone
response surface is much stronger evidence than a grid argmax, and it is what separates this
from a fit:

| | incumbent | h2000_t750 | change |
|---|---|---|---|
| traded-day + % | 46.0 | **50.9** | **+4.9 pp** |
| week + % | 58.3 | 59.8 | +1.5 pp |
| longest weekly losing streak | 8 | **5** | −3 |
| median week | $455 | **$767** | **+69 %** |
| weekly $ at fixed drawdown | $1,475 | $1,445 | **−2 %** |
| mean of 5 deepest drawdowns | $14,266 | $12,455 | **−13 %** |

It is still a hindsight pick and it is **not adopted here**. What would license it is a
walk-forward that only ever moves along the monotone direction rather than choosing freely from
216 — a constrained selector, which is a different and much smaller hypothesis than the one S1
tested and lost.

## 6. What this closes and opens
- `CLOSED`: free selection over the outer parameters. It is worse than random.
- `OPEN`: a **constrained** move along the two monotone axes (halt up, target down), and the
  selection-free **aggregate over the halt**, which captures much of the same gain with no
  parameter chosen. Both need their own wave.
- `RECORDED`: the consistency/production exchange rate on this object is about **$860 per extra
  green day**. That is the owner's dial, and it is now labelled.
