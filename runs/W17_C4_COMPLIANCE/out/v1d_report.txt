PART 2c -- THE 16:00 SPIKE: B-MOM's HARDCODED 15:57 SHUTDOWN
==============================================================================
BmomBar() zeroes bmomPos at hm>=155700 and never revives it until the next 09:33,
so B == 0 on EVERY bar from 15:57 to the close. B carries weight 2.83 -- 94% of the
3.0 entry threshold -- so removing it is a large, deterministic, once-a-day jump in M.

  B was non-zero on the preceding 15:54 bar          : 1027

  |M|>=3 at 15:57 if B had NOT been zeroed           : 642

PART 2d -- WHY T FREEZES: realised 3-min move vs the member stop distances
==============================================================================
Member m flips only when price travels S_m = clamp(VolMult_m * sigma, 40..1200 ticks)
from its anchor, with VolMult in {6,8,...,30} and sigma = a causal 460-bar mean of
|close-to-close|. Pooled sigma over the dev window = 6.50 NQ points, so
S ranges about 39 to 195 points (clamped to [10, 300] points).
