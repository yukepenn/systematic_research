=== 3. W99's ORACLE-OVER-FAMILY ($13,981/session): WITHDRAWN, and my first control for
===    it was MIS-SPECIFIED. What a router can actually reach is measured causally.
==========================================================================================================================
    one-shot best-of-12 chosen PER GROUP, ex post ....... $    9,239/session
    a RANDOM rule choice per group (mean over the 12) ... $     -213/session
    the whole gap, $9,452/session, is a max over 12 series and is SELECTION.

    `CORRECTION` My first control permuted each rule's outcomes across groups
    INDEPENDENTLY. That destroys the correlation between rules, and a max over 12
    independent series is much larger than a max over 12 correlated ones - so the null
    came back ABOVE the real value ($23,325 vs $9,239) and the 'excess' was -152 %. The
    control was inflating the null, not the statistic. A common permutation is degenerate
    for a sum. There is no useful null for an ex-post max; the statistic itself is the
    wrong instrument. It is withdrawn rather than repaired.

    THE REPLACEMENT - a CAUSAL router: in each segment, take the rule with the best
    trailing record IN THAT SEGMENT over the last K sessions. Fully executable.
