======================================================================================================================
=== 4. WHAT WOULD MAKE THIS ASKABLE
======================================================================================================================
    Against the LANE-SCALED bar of $194.06, the event-session MDE is
    $1,896.67 - short by a factor of 9.8x.
    Power scales with N, so the effective N must rise by 96x - from 71 event sessions to about 6,782.
    At roughly 2.6 scheduled events per month that is on the order of
    215 YEARS of additional calendar.

    That number is a reductio, not a plan. It is printed to make the shape of the problem
    explicit: the constraint is the CALENDAR, and no modelling choice moves it.

    Adding event TYPES (PPI, retail sales, claims, PCE, GDP, ISM, auctions) is the only
    lever that does not require waiting. It is a data-acquisition question, not a research
    question, and it belongs in OWNER_QUEUE rather than in a wave.
