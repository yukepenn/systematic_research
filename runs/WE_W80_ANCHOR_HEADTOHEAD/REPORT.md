## 4. The tension this wave surfaced, stated rather than buried

X9a beats P1 **outside** P1's development window and loses **inside** it:

| | X9a vs P1 |
|---|---|
| 2006–2021 (4,279 sessions, never used) | **+53 %** (1.41 vs 0.92 pts/session) |
| 2023 | worse ($157 vs $302/wk) |
| 2024 | better ($2,150 vs $1,915) |
| 2025 | **much worse** ($784 vs $2,265) |
| 2026 YTD | **much better** ($1,595 vs $412) |
| W76's 46 held-out sessions | **+$16,702 vs −$20,686** |

**P1's development window is 2022–2026. X9a wins on both sides of it and loses in the middle.**
That is the signature you would expect if the 09:31 anchor is fitted to the middle of the
development sample — and the rolling test, computed entirely *within* the modern window, cannot
see it, because that window is dominated by the years P1 was chosen on.

**This does not overturn the verdict.** The preregistered rule was written before the read and
X9a fails two of three gates. But it is the strongest reason in the campaign to keep testing the
anchor, and it is recorded as such.

## 5. Concentration check (method rule 29), applied to the held-out window

| | net | **minus top-3 days** | median traded day | positive days |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| P1 | −$20,686 | **−$21,237** | −$1,447 | 19.6 % |
| X9a | +$16,702 | **−$15,658** | −$1,359 | 21.7 % |

X9a's forward advantage is **194 % concentrated in three of forty-six sessions**, and its median
traded day is essentially the same as P1's. **The forward signal on its own is weak** — the deep
run is what carries the evidence, not those nine weeks.

## 6. Harness

`we_fastctx.py` — a vectorised `build_context`, written because the originals contain
`idx[sid == s]` loops that are O(n × n_sess) and would need ~2.8 × 10¹⁰ operations on the deep
substrate. **Verified bit-for-bit against the originals on every array**, on a modern slice and
again on a 2010 slice before the deep run. An earlier draft failed that check — its
time-of-day norm rolled over *sessions* where the original rolls over *observations*, and gated
on the count inside the window where the original gates on the whole history. Both were fixed
before use; without the check the entire deep run would have been silently wrong.
