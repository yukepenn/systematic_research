R2  EFFECTIVE SPREAD (definition given in launch instruction): effective spread at second s of
    minute m = quoted spread at that instant. Instant grid = every exact wall-clock second
    (.000) from session open 18:00:00 ET (previous calendar day) through 16:59:59 ET of the
    session date — 82,800 instants/session, naive ET wall-clock (repo convention; no session
    spans a DST changeover because changeovers fall 02:00 Sunday inside the nonexistent
    Saturday-evening session). Prevailing quote = last bid event and last ask event with
    event_time <= instant; staleness cap 5.000 s PER SIDE (EXEC01 R4); crossed books (ask<bid)
    invalid; locked (ask==bid) kept. Instants after store end (min of last bid/ask event time)
    are invalid (after_store_end). Spread in ticks = (ask-bid)/0.25.

R3  SECOND-OF-MINUTE BIN = instant.second (0..59). The hh:mm:00.000 instant is exactly where a

R4  PRIMARY GATE STATISTIC. S(s) = pooled sample-weighted MEAN spread (ticks) over all valid
    grid samples in bin s (mean is the money-relevant statistic; medians printed as
    secondaries). Baseline B = pooled mean over ALL bins s NOT IN {0,30} ("mid-minute
    seconds" read conservatively as the full 58-second complement: near-boundary seconds that
    may share the elevation RAISE the baseline and make the gate HARDER to pass).
    Penalty P0 = S(0)-B, P30 = S(30)-B. PASS requires BOTH P0 >= 0.25 tick AND P30 >= 0.25
    tick (spec "sec-0 and sec-30" read as a conjunction — conservative). Session-clustered
    SEs: per-session penalties P0_j, P30_j (same formula within session j, sessions with all
    62 quantities defined), SE = sd/sqrt(n_sess); MDE(95%) = 1.96 x SE. Bin event-counts and
    MDEs are printed BEFORE the verdict, per launch rule 7.
