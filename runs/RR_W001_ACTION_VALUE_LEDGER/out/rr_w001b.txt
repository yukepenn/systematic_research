    >> UNITS DEFECT IN THE SPEC, found and fixed BEFORE this ledger was read.
    The spec compares an MDE for a Q5-Q1 CONTRAST against a PER-DECISION materiality bar.
    Those are not the same unit: a router that abstains on the bottom quintile does not
    earn the contrast, it earns roughly 0.2 x (the amount by which Q1 sits below the mean).
    Under a symmetric contrast that is 0.1 x MDE_contrast per decision. Both readings are
    printed. The LITERAL spec reading is the binding gate; the corrected reading is
    reported beside it because the gate's stated RATIONALE - 'if the smallest contrast we
    could detect is larger than the improvement we would need to earn, the question is
    UNDERPOWERED' - is a statement about policy value, not about a raw contrast.
    G3  LITERAL  : MDE_contrast $564.63  vs bar $13.93   -> FAIL
    G3' CORRECTED: smallest DETECTABLE per-decision policy gain $56.46  vs bar $13.93   -> FAIL

=== 11b. GATE INTEGRITY - what an adversarial audit found wrong with these gates.
=== Recorded here rather than by quietly restating the gates. No coded clause was changed.
============================================================================================================================
    G4 IS VOID. It cannot fail. Its statistic is minus the sum of the most-negative 20 % of
    action values, and 59.03 % of all action values are negative, so every
    bucket's bottom quintile is entirely negative and the statistic is positive by
    arithmetic. Largest element of each bucket's bottom quintile:
        2022: $     -609.36   (all negative: True)
        2023: $     -508.72   (all negative: True)
        2024: $     -418.72   (all negative: True)
        2025: $     -484.36   (all negative: True)
        2026: $   -1,028.72   (all negative: True)
    '5 of 5' therefore carries ZERO evidential content. This is exactly the un-failable-gate
    error the spec's own why_the_raw_oracle_is_NOT_the_gate section forbids. G4 is recorded
    VOID, not PASS. Stability is NOT established by this wave.

    G2 PASSES ITS CODED CLAUSE AND FAILS ITS OWN RATIONALE. The clause measures the top 5 %
    share of TOTAL ABSOLUTE action value (39.28 %). The rationale asks whether 'essentially
    all of it sits in ~100 events' - which is a question about the SUM a router would earn:
        top 107 events by |action value| contribute $363,787 = 104.9 % of the $346,915 SUM
        the remaining 2,024 events sum to $-16,872 (mean $-8.34 per decision)
        single largest |action value| event = $42,980 = 12.39 % of the total sum
    By the economically relevant measure the concentration condition IS met. G2 is recorded
    PASS-ON-CLAUSE / FAIL-ON-RATIONALE and must not be quoted as a clean pass.

    G3 IS TOO GENEROUS BY sqrt(2). The 0.1x conversion is valid only if Q1 and Q5 are
    constrained symmetric about the mean, which borrows precision from Q5 that a router
    abstaining on Q1 does not have. Assumption-free: MDE_gain = 0.2 * Z80 * sd / sqrt(neff/5).
        symmetric (as coded)   $     56.46
        assumption-free        $     79.85   = sqrt(2) x the above
        materiality bar        $     13.93
    G3 fails under BOTH (5.7x the bar assumption-free). The failure is robust.
