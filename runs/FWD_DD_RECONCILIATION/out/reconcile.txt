=== THE REAL CAUSE: WEEK-BOUNDARY CONVENTION, not cost model
============================================================================================================
    W103/W110 : ISO week keyed on SESSION DATE      maxDD $  22,930.67
    RR_W003   : Sunday-ending date label            maxDD $  24,212.92
    same trades, totals agree to $79.65, but maxDD differs by $1,282.25 (5.6 %)

    Max drawdown is SENSITIVE TO BUCKETING because where a week boundary falls decides
    whether a run of losses lands inside one bucket or is split across two. Both series are
    legitimate aggregations of the same trades. Neither is defective.

    >>> CONSEQUENCE FOR THE FORWARD PROTOCOL: the forward read MUST bucket weeks the same
    >>> way the bands were derived - ISO week on session date - or it will compare a
    >>> drawdown against a threshold built on a different convention.
