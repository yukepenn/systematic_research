R1. Diagnostic window identification (required first step). Per the frozen diagnostic

    Decile edges for session i are computed from D over the 252 immediately preceding
    qualifying sessions (i-252 .. i-1, strictly prior — causal; recomputed here, NOT
    reused from the diagnostic's within-era full-sample deciles). Exactly 252 prior

    excluded (strict inequalities — conservative, fewer events). No threshold search.
