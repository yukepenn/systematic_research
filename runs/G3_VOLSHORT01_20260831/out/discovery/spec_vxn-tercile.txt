VERDICT: DEAD

Reason, stated plainly and without dressing:

  1. THE SIGN IS BACKWARDS. The mechanism predicts NEGATIVE intraday drift in the
     high implied-vol state. Measured, it is +1.7768 NQ points per session --
     POSITIVE, and HIGHER than the mid state's +0.4816. The short leg
     therefore earns -1.7768 points per session BEFORE costs. It is not a small
     edge swamped by costs; it is the wrong side of the trade.
  2. It loses by a wide margin at every cost line, including the $4.36 commission-only
     FLOOR ($-34,231), so no execution improvement rescues it.
  3. The episode-block CI CONTAINS zero (p=0.1686) and the rate-matched
     circular-shift placebo puts the observed value at the 49th percentile
     (p=0.5069) -- i.e. INDISTINGUISHABLE from shorting an arbitrarily aligned mask
     of the same rate and the same episode structure. There is no signal here at all,
     in either direction; there is a cost drag on top of a slightly adverse sign.
  4. net(R) - net(F) = $-48,208 on 2,807 sessions. The router IS distinguishable
     from the filter -- but in the wrong direction: the short leg destroys $48,208
     relative to simply removing the sessions. 'Distinguishable' was never the goal;
     PROFITABLY distinguishable was, and G3/G4/G5 all fail.

  AND the exposure-gate reading fails too, so this is not a case of 'wrong arm chosen':
     BASE always-long   net@$20.65 = $    36,650  ($13.057/valid session)
     F filter non_high  net@$20.65 = $    23,878  ($8.507/valid session)
     Removing the high-VXN sessions LOSES $12,772 versus doing nothing. High
     implied vol is not an intraday short trigger and it is not an exposure gate either.

  NOT PROMOTED, and stated so explicitly: the fact that the high state's drift is
  positive is NOT a long candidate. Its low state is higher still, its CI is wide, and
  flipping a sign after seeing the sign is exactly the post-hoc move this repo bans.
  Nothing in this file may be reframed as a LONG proposal without its own preregistration.
