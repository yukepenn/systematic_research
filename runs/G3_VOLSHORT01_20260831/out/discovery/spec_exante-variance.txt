5. RATE-MATCHED PLACEBOS FOR THE SHORT ARM (net $/session at the $20.65 PRIMARY line)
====================================================================================================
  The binding null is a CIRCULAR SHIFT of the high-state mask against returns: it keeps
  the episode clustering of the mask AND the serial structure of returns. The i.i.d.
  placebo is printed only to show how much too easy an independent-draw bar is.

  spec                    obs $/sess  circ mean  circ p2.5  circ p97.5  pct>=obs  iid pct
  SPEC-A  iv_var(VXN)        -16.942    -16.848    -34.113       0.677     0.501    0.503
  SPEC-B1 vrp_rth            -22.163    -17.175    -35.777       0.612     0.702    0.667
  SPEC-B2 vrp_cc             -22.600    -16.962    -36.467       1.588     0.727    0.679

  READ: `pct>=obs` is the one-sided p of the observed short-arm net against the
  dependence-preserving placebo. A short arm that cannot beat a rate-matched random
  short is dead by the same argument that closed the ten-for-ten anti-filter family.

  *** THE INTERPRETIVE POINT THAT DECIDES THIS SPECIFICATION ***
  The placebo distribution is CENTRED WELL BELOW ZERO. A rate-matched RANDOM short,
  carrying no information at all, loses roughly the same per session as the vol-
  triggered short. So 'net(S) < 0 with a CI excluding zero' is NOT evidence about the
  vol state -- it is the $20.65 round turn plus the long-run upward drift of NQ being
  paid on the wrong side. Testing net(S) against ZERO answers the wrong question.

8. WHAT THIS MEANS
####################################################################################################
  Mechanism prediction: high ex-ante implied variance -> NEGATIVE intraday drift.
  Observed high-state intraday drift, SPEC-A : +1.742 NQ points per session
  Observed high-state intraday drift, SPEC-B2: +2.622 NQ points per session
  Cost of a full-session round turn at the primary line: 1.0325 points ($20.65).

  The short arm must overcome BOTH the sign of the observed drift AND 1.03 points of
  cost. Read the S rows above for the magnitude; do not read the FLOOR column as a
  headline.

  1. THE SIGN IS BACKWARDS. In 14 of the 14 non-inverted robustness
     variants the high state's intraday drift is POSITIVE, so the short arm is on the
     wrong side before costs. The high-vol NQ session is not where intraday drift is
     negative; it is where intraday drift is LARGE AND POSITIVE with a huge standard
     deviation (sd ~91 pts vs ~51 in the low state). High implied vol buys volatility,
     not a direction.
  2. THE VRP DOES NOT RESCUE IT. vrp_cc is the least redundant construction available
     here (75.9% state agreement with the plain iv_var tercile, 45.7% with realised
     variance), so it genuinely separates the PRICE of variance from its LEVEL -- and it
     makes the short arm WORSE, not better: -2.622 pts/short against -1.742.
  3. THE ROUTER IS NOT A FILTER IN A COSTUME -- IT IS WORSE THAN THE FILTER. net(R) -
     net(F) == net(S) identically, and net(S) is reliably negative, so the short leg is
     not merely adding nothing; it is destroying what the filter keeps. On SPEC-B2 the
     filter earns +$3.23/session at the primary line and the router loses $19.37.
  4. AND THE FILTER IS NOTHING EITHER. F_non earns LESS than BASE always-long
     ($3.23 vs $13.06 per session on SPEC-B2), so removing high-vol sessions removes
     good sessions. This is the eleventh member of the closed anti-filter family, not
     an escape from it.
  5. WHAT WOULD HAVE TO BE TRUE FOR THE MECHANISM TO SURVIVE: the overnight-vs-intraday
     split of the variance premium may still be real, but on NQ 2010-2021 the
     compensation shows up on the LONG side intraday in the high-vol state. If any
     version of this idea is ever revisited, the sign to test is LONG-when-vol-is-high,
     and that is a different (and heavily discovery-contaminated) claim which this run
     does not license anyone to freeze.

  SAMPLE LIMITATION, stated: VXN begins 2009-09, so SPEC-A/B1/B2 never see 2008. The
  VIX-based rows A' and B2' extend to 2006 and INCLUDE 2008; they agree (-$16.99 and
  -$14.71 per session). The absence of 2008 is not what is killing this.
