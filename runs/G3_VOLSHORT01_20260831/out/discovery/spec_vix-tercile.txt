  HOW TO READ THIS COMPARISON -- and how NOT to.
  Both numbers are NEGATIVE and both CIs straddle zero. Neither index produces a
  signed short edge, so the question the comparison was designed to answer ('is the
  effect NQ-specific?') is VACUOUS: there is no effect whose locus could be argued

  What the two rows DO say is that the INVERSE of the claim -- long the high state --
  is larger under the broad-market VIX (+3.01 pts/session) than under the
  NQ-native VXN (+1.78) on the matched sample. If anything survived here it would be
  a BROAD-MARKET risk phenomenon, not an NQ one. That inverse is NOT a candidate:
  flipping the sign after seeing the result is exactly the move this repo's method
  forbids, the episode CI on it straddles zero, and the long-the-high-state leg is
  already inside the always-long BASE, whose own CI also straddles zero.

[11] VERDICT
====================================================================================================
  DEAD.

  The mechanism predicts NEGATIVE intraday drift in the high implied-variance state.
  Observed mean intraday drift in the high-VIX tercile is +1.5894 points --
  i.e. the SHORT leg loses 1.5894 points per trade GROSS, before it
  pays the 1.0325-point round turn. The sign is INVERTED relative to the claim.
  Net(S) at the PRIMARY line: $-62,874 over 1,199 shorts = $-52.44 per trade.

  Four independent ways this dies, none of which is a magnitude quibble:
   1. SIGN. The high-VIX tercile's intraday drift is +1.589 pts, not
      negative. It is even positive in log terms (1.03 bp), so it is not a
      point-scaling artefact. The proposed trigger points the wrong way.
   2. NO INFORMATION. Against a rate-matched CIRCULAR-SHIFT null that preserves the
      episode clustering exactly, the observed short sits at the 37.7th percentile --
      an unremarkable draw. The VIX tercile carries no signed intraday information at
      all; the whole short family merely fights NQ's +1.21 pt/session intraday drift.
   3. NOT ONE EPISODE. Removing COVID (2020-02-21..2020-07-17, the single worst block
      for the short) still leaves -0.6735 pts/trade -- still short of the
      1.0325-pt round turn by 1.71 pts. The short leg wins in only 18/59 episodes.
   4. NO MAGNITUDE HEADROOM. Even the FLOOR cost line ($4.36, commission only, which
      is never a headline) leaves net(S) at $-43,343. There is no cost
      assumption under which this arm is positive.

  The filter arm is not a consolation prize either: F(low_only) nets $6.03/session at
  PRIMARY but its 95% episode-block CI is [-$12.42, $30.99] -- it does not exclude
  zero, and F(non_high) is -$0.11/session. That is the repo's closed anti-filter
  family behaving exactly as it has ten times before.

  DISCOVERY_CONTAMINATED. No part of this is a result, and no part of it justifies
  spending the one-shot 2022-01-01 -> 2026-07-31 confirmation window.
