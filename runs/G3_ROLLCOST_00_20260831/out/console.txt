and the observed safe re-enable is ~1 day after it. That is a ~9-day no-new-entry window,
4x per year. This measures what the book HISTORICALLY EARNED inside those windows.

================================================================================================
WHAT THIS IS AND IS NOT
================================================================================================
  IS: the FOREGONE amount under the current roll PROCEDURE (stop -> wait out the window ->
      redeploy after the rollover). It is an UPPER BOUND on the cost: some of it would be
      recovered by trades just outside the window, and 2022 is negative (-32.4%), so the
      blackout HELPED in the old regime.
  IS NOT: an unavoidable cost, and NOT a signal. The guard exists to stop the book holding
      an expiring contract - that is correct and must not be weakened. What is avoidable is
      SITTING OUT. Redeploying onto the NEXT contract at or just before blockNewEntriesFrom
      resolves a NEW rollover date (December, for NQ 12-26) and no block applies.
  COST OF THE FIX: trading the back month for ~2 days before the natural volume crossover,
      i.e. a wider spread on a handful of sessions. That is a small, measurable price for
      ~9 days x 4 per year of restored trading.
  NOT A RECOMMENDATION TO ACT TODAY: changing the roll procedure is an OWNER decision and
      the 2026-09 roll is the first opportunity. No live action is taken by this run.
