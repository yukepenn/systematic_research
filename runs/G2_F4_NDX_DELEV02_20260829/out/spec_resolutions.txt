R7. SESSION ALIGNMENT (index calendar day -> futures session) + HOLIDAY MISMATCHES
    Index date D maps to the futures session LABELED D — by the END-stamp convention that is exactly
    the session whose RTH (09:30-16:00 ET) falls on calendar date D. Mismatch cases:
    (a) index date with NO futures U-session labeled D (futures holiday or 13:00 half-day while the
        index printed a close): an EVENT on such a date is UNTRADEABLE under the frozen policy (the
        event-day futures RTH close is required for the gap veto; no alternative session is guessed).
        Counted separately in the printed table (untradeable_align) and in events.csv.
    (b) futures U-session with no index date (index holiday, futures open): the session still counts
        as a U-session for entry / exit / the 3-session hold (the horizon is defined in FUTURES
        sessions, per spec: "exit at futures RTH close 3 sessions later"). It can host no event and
        no control (those are index-day objects).
    Counts of both mismatch types over the window are printed in contract.txt.

R9. POLICY (frozen; futures P&L in POINTS on the owned substrate)
    For an event on index date D mapped to futures U-session s at U-position fu:

    fu+3 exists with label <= 2026-05-31, AND the fu+1 09:31 open exists (finite). Otherwise
